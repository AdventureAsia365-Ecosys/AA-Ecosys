**Overview**  
Escape the ordinary on a captivating 3-day journey through Vietnam’s natural wonders. Begin with an 8:00 pickup in Hanoi, travelling to Ninh Binh to glide along Tam Coc’s serene waterways by bamboo boat, followed by cycling through emerald rice fields. Spend the night in a local homestay, savouring authentic Vietnamese hospitality and a home-cooked dinner. Day two takes you to Ha Long Bay, where an overnight cruise awaits. Paddle through the tranquil waters of Luon Cave by kayak, then master regional dishes in a fun onboard cooking class. As dusk falls, unwind on the deck before retiring to your cabin. On the final morning, greet the day with tai chi amid breathtaking bay vistas, explore the awe-inspiring Sung Sot Cave, and enjoy a leisurely brunch before returning to Hanoi by 16:00. With small groups (max 12) and moderate activity levels, this adventure blends culture, nature and relaxation seamlessly.  

VI: Hành trình 3 ngày khám phá Ninh Bình và Vịnh Hạ Long: chèo thuyền tre Tam Cốc, đạp xe qua cánh đồng lúa, ngủ homestay, kayak hang Luồn, lớp học nấu ăn và thăm hang Sửng Sốt, nhóm tối đa 12 người.
