Discover the stunning landscapes of northern Vietnam on our three-day Ninh Binh and Ha Long Bay adventure. After an early pick-up from Hanoi, glide along the Tam Coc river in a traditional bamboo boat, then cycle peacefully through serene rice fields, enjoying a local homestay overnight. The journey continues as you transfer to the magnificent Ha Long Bay, boarding a traditional junk for an overnight cruise. Paddle a kayak into the tranquil waters of Luon Cave, master local flavours in a cooking class, and relax on deck. Your final morning begins with rejuvenating tai chi and exploring the vast Sung Sot Cave, before a farewell brunch as you cruise back to land. This small-group tour is perfect for travellers with a moderate fitness level.
