Explore the natural beauty of northern Vietnam on this 3-day adventure. You'll begin with a bamboo boat ride along the tranquil Tam Coc river and a leisurely cycle through picturesque rice fields, staying overnight in a local homestay. The next day, you'll cruise through Ha Long Bay, kayaking at Luon Cave and enjoying a cooking class on board. The final day brings tai chi on the sun deck and a visit to stunning Sung Sot Cave. With a maximum group size of 12, you'll experience an intimate and immersive journey.

VI: Khám phá vẻ đẹp tự nhiên của miền Bắc Việt Nam trong hành trình 3 ngày, từ chèo thuyền tre đến tham quan vịnh Hạ Long.
