Discover the best of Northern Vietnam on this captivating three-day journey. Departing Hanoi at 08:00, you will travel to Ninh Binh to drift along the Tam Coc river on a traditional bamboo boat and cycle through scenic rice fields before enjoying dinner and a homestay overnight. The adventure continues as you board an overnight Ha Long Bay cruise. Try kayaking at Luon Cave, join a cooking class, and spend the night on the water. Start your final day with Tai Chi on the sun deck, explore Sung Sot Cave, and enjoy brunch before returning to Hanoi at approximately 16:00. With a maximum group size of 12 and requiring moderate fitness, this tour offers a perfect blend of activity and relaxation.

VI: Khám phá vẻ đẹp Ninh Binh và Hạ Long trong chuyến đi 3 ngày bao gồm chèo thuyền, đạp xe, du thuyền và các hoạt động thể thao thú vị.
