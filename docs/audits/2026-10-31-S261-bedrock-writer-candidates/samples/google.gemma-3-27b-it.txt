## Ninh Binh & Ha Long Bay: A 3-Day Northern Vietnam Discovery

Embark on a captivating three-day journey exploring the stunning landscapes of Ninh Binh and Ha Long Bay. Beginning with an 8:00 am pick-up from Hanoi, we’ll travel to Ninh Binh for a tranquil bamboo boat ride along the Tam Coc river and a delightful cycle through picturesque rice paddies. Enjoy an overnight stay in a local homestay, with dinner included. 

The adventure continues as we journey to Ha Long Bay, boarding an overnight cruise to explore the iconic bay. Discover hidden caves by kayak at Luon Cave and participate in an onboard cooking class. Finish with tai chi on the sun deck & a visit to Sung Sot Cave before returning to Hanoi around 4:00 pm. Small group size (maximum 12) and a moderate level of fitness are required.

VI: Chuyến đi ba ngày này khám phá vẻ đẹp của Ninh Bình và Vịnh Hạ Long, bao gồm chèo thuyền, đạp xe, chèo kayak và lớp học nấu ăn.
