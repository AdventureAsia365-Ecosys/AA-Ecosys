**Discover Ninh Binh & Ha Long Bay – A Three-Day Journey**

Experience the serene beauty of Vietnam’s iconic landscapes on this unforgettable three-day adventure. Begin in Ninh Binh, where you’ll glide through Tam Coc’s emerald waters on a traditional bamboo boat, then cycle past lush rice fields. Enjoy a warm homestay dinner, immersing yourself in local culture. On day two, journey to Ha Long Bay, boarding an exquisite cruise for an evening of kayaking at Luon Cave and an onboard cooking class, savouring fresh, regional flavours. Wake to tai chi on the sun deck, explore the magnificent Sung Sot Cave, and indulge in a leisurely brunch before returning to Hanoi. With a small group of up to 12, this tour blends gentle exploration with authentic experiences.

VI: Khám phá Ninh Bình và Hạ Long trong 3 ngày—chèo thuyền trên sông Tam Cốc, đạp xe qua cánh đồng lúa, ngủ qua đêm trên du thuyền và trải nghiệm cuộc sống địa phương.
