Embark on a captivating 3-day journey through the serene landscapes of Ninh Binh and the majestic Ha Long Bay. Begin with a comfortable pick-up from Hanoi at 8:00, leading you to the enchanting rice fields of Ninh Binh. Here, glide along the Tam Coc river on a traditional bamboo boat and pedal through lush countryside on a cycling adventure. Enjoy a welcoming dinner at a charming local homestay. The next day, transfer to Ha Long where you'll board a comfortable overnight cruise. Experience the thrill of kayaking through the mystical Luon Cave and participate in a hands-on cooking class to prepare a delicious dinner. Conclude your adventure with a soothing tai chi session on the sun deck, a visit to the impressive Sung Sot Cave, and a leisurely brunch before returning to Hanoi by 16:00. This moderate tour, suited for groups of up to 12, promises an unforgettable experience.

VI: Tour 3 ngày này đưa bạn khám phá vẻ đẹp yên bình của Ninh Binh và Vịnh Ha Long qua những trải nghiệm độc đáo như đua thuyền tre trên sông Tam Coc, đạp xe qua cánh đồng lúa và học nấu ăn trên du thuyền.
