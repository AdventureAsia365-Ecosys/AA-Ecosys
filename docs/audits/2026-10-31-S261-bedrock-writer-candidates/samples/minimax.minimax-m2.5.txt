Embark on a captivating three-day journey through two of northern Vietnam's most spectacular destinations. Your adventure begins with an early morning pick-up from Hanoi at 8:00, travelling to the serene landscapes of Ninh Binh. Drift along the tranquil Tam Coc river on a traditional bamboo boat, then explore the surrounding rice fields by bicycle before settling into a local homestay for dinner. Day two takes you to Ha Long Bay, where you'll board an overnight cruise and paddle through the enchanting Luon Cave by kayak. Learn the secrets of Vietnamese cuisine during an on-board cooking class. Rise early for tai chi on the sun deck, explore the magnificent Sung Sot Cave, and enjoy brunch before returning to Hanoi by approximately 16:00. With small groups of up to twelve participants and a moderate fitness level required, this tour offers a perfectly balanced blend of culture, nature, and adventure.

VI: Trải nghiệm ba ngày tuyệt vời khám phá Ninh Binh và Vịnh Hạ Long với homestay, du thuyền qua đêm, chèo kayak, học nấu ăn và tập thái cực.
