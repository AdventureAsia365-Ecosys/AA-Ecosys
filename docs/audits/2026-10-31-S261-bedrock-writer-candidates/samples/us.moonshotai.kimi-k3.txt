**3 Days Ninh Binh & Ha Long Bay**

Two of northern Vietnam's most loved destinations in one seamless three-day escape. Day one begins with an 8:00 pick-up in Hanoi before we drive to Ninh Binh, where you'll drift along the Tam Coc river in a traditional bamboo boat and cycle through rice fields, then settle into a welcoming homestay with dinner included. On day two we travel to Ha Long Bay and board an overnight cruise: kayak at Luon Cave, pick up new skills in a cooking class on board, and enjoy dinner before sleeping on the water. Your final morning starts with tai chi on the sun deck, followed by a visit to Sung Sot Cave and brunch on board, returning to Hanoi around 16:00. With groups capped at 12 and moderate fitness required, it's the ideal soft adventure.
