Embark on a captivating 3-day journey through Vietnam’s most breathtaking landscapes. Start with a morning pick-up in Hanoi and drive to Ninh Binh, where you’ll glide through serene waterways on a traditional bamboo boat and pedal past lush rice fields before settling into a local homestay with dinner included. Day two takes you to Halong Bay, where you’ll board a cruise, paddle through the mysterious Luon Cave, and learn to cook Vietnamese dishes onboard. Rise with the sunrise for tai chi on deck, then explore the majestic Sung Sot Cave before enjoying a final brunch. Return to Hanoi by 16:00 on day three. Designed for moderate fitness levels and limited to 12 guests, this tour offers authentic, intimate experiences.
