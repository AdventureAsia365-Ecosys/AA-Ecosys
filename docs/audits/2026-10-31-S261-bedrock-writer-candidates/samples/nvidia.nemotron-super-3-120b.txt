Explore the contrasting landscapes of northern Vietnam on this 3-day adventure: cycle through emerald rice paddies, glide along the tranquil Tam Coc River by bamboo boat, and experience the magic of Ha Long Bay overnight. Begin with a scenic drive from Hanoi at 8:00, followed by a peaceful boat ride and countryside cycling, ending with a homestay dinner in Ninh Binh. The next day brings a journey to Ha Long Bay, where you’ll kayak through the hidden Luon Cave, join a hands-on cooking class, and dine under the stars aboard your cruise. Wake to tai chi on the sun deck, explore the awe-inspiring Sung Sot Cave, and enjoy a leisurely brunch before returning to Hanoi by 16:00. Designed for up to 12 travellers with a moderate fitness level, this tour blends active discovery with authentic local experiences.  
VI: Chu trình 3 ngày khám phá Ninh Bình và Vịnh Hạ Long với đạp xe, lottorng, kayaking và lớp học nấu ăn trên thuyền.
