Discover the scenic beauty of northern Vietnam on this 3-day soft-adventure tour. Begin with a scenic drive from Hanoi to Ninh Binh, where you’ll glide through lush landscapes on a traditional bamboo boat along the Tam Coc river and cycle past tranquil rice fields. Spend the night in a local homestay with dinner included. Journey to Ha Long Bay the next day, boarding a comfortable cruise for kayaking in the serene Luon Cave and learning to prepare Vietnamese dishes in a hands-on cooking class. Wake to tai chi at sunrise, explore the impressive Sung Sot Cave, and enjoy brunch before returning to Hanoi. Perfect for small groups of up to 12, this tour suits travellers with moderate fitness.

VI: Trải nghiệm 3 ngày khám phá Ninh Bình và vịnh Hạ Long với đi thuyền tre, đạp xe, kayak và học nấu ăn trên du thuyền.
