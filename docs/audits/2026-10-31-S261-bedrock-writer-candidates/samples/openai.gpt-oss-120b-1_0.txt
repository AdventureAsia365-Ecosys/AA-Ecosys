Join us for a relaxed three‑day soft‑adventure through Ninh Binh’s emerald waterways and Ha Long Bay’s iconic limestone seascape. On day one, an 8:00 pick‑up in Hanoi sets off a scenic drive to Ninh Binh, where you’ll glide downstream on a bamboo boat through Tam Coc, then cycle along golden rice fields before settling into a welcoming homestay for dinner. Day two continues with a transfer to Ha Long, boarding an overnight cruise that offers kayaking in Luon Cave, a hands‑on cooking class, and a leisurely dinner under the stars. The final morning begins with tai chi on the sun deck, a visit to spectacular Sung Sot Cave, a brunch on board and a comfortable return to Hanoi by 16:00. The small‑group format (max 12) suits travellers of moderate fitness.
