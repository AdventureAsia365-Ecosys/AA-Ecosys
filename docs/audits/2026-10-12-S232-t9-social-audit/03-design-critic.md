- **Category-based exemption makes the judge grade itself.** In A1, the labelers assigned the categories, so "exempt categories are never real violations" is true by construction. In F9 v2 the judge will pick the category, and code will drop anything tagged exempt. A real scene-detail or trip-promise claim that the judge tags `place_background` or `paraphrase_supported` would then pass silently, and nothing measures that. E1 needs a category-accuracy check. It should report how many of the 48 real violations F9 v2 tags as exempt, and the gate should allow 0 of them in the trip_promise, location and number categories.

- **C3 and C4 contradict each other on paraphrase, so Jev's added value is unclear.** Step 1 drops paraphrase in code, yet C4 expects Jev to clear "mostly the 15 paraphrase cases" that survive step 1. Once F9 v2 filters, Jev only sees mis-categorized claims and real violations. E2 should therefore be scored on the simulated set left after step 1, not on all 127 sentences. The 127 include many easy exempt sentences, which would inflate the clear count and the precision. Run E2 (about $0.02) in S233, before F9 v2 is finalized, so the team can decide whether both mechanisms are needed.

- **The C7 thresholds are too small to show a 5% wrong-clear rate.** With about 15 offline clears, 0 errors still leaves a 95% upper bound of about 20%. The live rule "≤1 wrong clear across ≥40 flagged sentences" counts flags, not clears; 1 wrong out of about 10 clears is a 10% error rate. The rule should be stated in clears, needs at least 40–60 clears pooled across offline and shadow, and the upper confidence bound should be reported.
