What's new in v2

Built in this mockup

  • One time range for the whole page (1 h, 6 h, 24 h, 7 d, custom). Steps follow it: 1 min, 5 min, 1 h, 6 h. Drag across a golden-signal chart with a mouse to zoom; Undo zoom goes back.
  • App filter in the sticky bar scopes every section; shared platform items always stay.
  • Filters on Needs attention (severity, open or acknowledged) and Events (severity, search). Each list says how many rows the filters hide.
  • Sort on services, job queues, AI connections and events: click a column header; on a phone use the Sort menu.
  • Legends are tick boxes: click to show or hide a series, double-click or only (or key O) to isolate it, Reset to go back.

 

  • Every chart shows its limits: warn as a dashed line, critical as a solid line with a shaded band, capacity and caps as marks; limits far above the data appear as “off scale” notes. A headroom line under each chart says how close each series is.
  • Compare with the previous period: faint dashed lines on the four golden-signal charts and the app request charts; deltas in the app tiles.
  • Uptime against an SLO (99.5 %) with error budget left per runtime.
  • Shareable view: range, filters, hidden series, sort and theme live in the URL hash; Copy link.
  • Changed: requests are per minute so ranges compare; the queue chart plots oldest wait, the value the 15 / 60 min limits apply to; 4xx warn is 10 % as in the advisory.
  • Ideas not built: saved views per role, notes pinned to a time on the charts, multi-window burn-rate alerts, pause auto-refresh in a background tab, tenant filter.
