# AA-820 — Implementation notes (root repo, lane: docs/legal)

Source: Linear AA-820 + `docs/audits/2026-10-14-S236-design-review/aup-2026-11-review.md` (S236).
Output: `docs/legal/tenant-terms-ai-clause-draft.md`, a draft for counsel. It is not approved text.

## Decisions
- **Stand-alone schedule.** The repo has no existing tenant Terms or contract template; a grep for
  "tenant terms", "terms of service" and "tenant agreement" found nothing. So the clause is written as
  a self-contained "AI Schedule" with `[●]` numbering and assumed defined terms ("Agreement", "Tenant",
  "Services", "Portal"). Counsel can attach it by amendment or fold it into the main agreement.
- **Product facts first (§1).** Every clause promise is checked against what the code does on CIS
  `origin/main` (1207fa5b):
  - portal notice text: `frontend/app/(tenant)/portal/_components/AiContentNotice.tsx`;
  - F19 authenticity gate: `services/acp_content_writing/authenticity.py`, `quality_gates.py`;
  - daily publish limit, default 20 per UTC day: AA-819 / migration 230;
  - suspend / resume / offboard: AA-797, `api/routers/admin.py`;
  - logging and retention: data standards §7 (`shared.llm_call_log` 180 d; `publish_log` and
    `audit_log` kept until a production window is set).

  The clause does not promise anything the product lacks. One forward-looking part is marked as
  such: a future chat (.4(c)). Takedown through stored credentials (.7(d), Q7) is **not** forward-looking:
  the AA-455/AA-560 admin force-unpublish already exists, so the clause gives it a contractual basis.
- **Review fixes (S237 adversarial review).** Product facts tightened against CIS main: F19 runs on
  T9 pieces only (T2 rewrites do not run it); the daily limit covers AA-published posts only; the
  tenant's review is expected, not enforced (API publish skips the portal); publish records name the
  tenant account or AA admin, not the individual approving user (.8(a) no longer promises that);
  suspend does not cancel queued jobs; the provider list in Q11 is to be confirmed from live routing.
- **The tenant carries the AI-label duty (.4(b)).** AA's reading is that the Anthropic policy does not
  require labels on marketing copy. Local law may require them, so the duty sits with the publisher,
  and Q1 and Q3 ask counsel to confirm.
- **Quotes are sourced, not re-fetched.** Policy quotations come from the S236 review (read 14/10). The
  header tells counsel to check them against the live AUP; the lane did not re-read the policy page.
- **Extra deliverables inside scope.** The doc includes a tenant notice draft (§4, for scope item 3)
  and proposed AA-Booking CONTEXT wording (§5, for scope item 4). Both are drafts in this file; neither
  is sent or applied.

## Tradeoffs
- Writing a full schedule rather than bullet points is more for counsel to rewrite. But it lets the
  business see the shape now, and counsel edits text faster than writing from a list.
- The questions name specific external rules (EU AI Act Art. 50, the FTC fake-review rule 16 CFR 465,
  Vietnam AI labelling) only as items "to check". The lane did not research them; asserting their
  content would be unverified legal advice.
- Retention numbers stay out of the clause body (drafting note only), so a future N4/L3 retention
  change does not force a Terms change. Counsel decides in Q8.
