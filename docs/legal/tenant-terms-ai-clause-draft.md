## 1. What counsel needs to know about the product

The clause has to match what the platform actually does. These are the facts as of 15/10/2026. Each
can be checked in the code.

| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| Who the tenant is | A travel business (a legal entity) that subscribes to AA's content platform ("AA-CIS") and signs in to the tenant portal. |
| What the AI produces for a tenant | (a) **Tour rewrites (T2):** AA's catalog tours rewritten in the tenant's brand voice. (b) **Social and blog pieces (T9):** blog, LinkedIn and Facebook copy about those tours. The text is generated by large language models. Claude (Anthropic) runs through AWS Bedrock, and other providers act as reviewers and judges (see Q11). |
| Who reviews | The tenant reviews each piece in the portal (T10) before anything is published. The portal shows this notice at review, publish and export: *"This content was generated with AI. Review it before publishing; you are responsible for what you publish."* |
| Who publishes | AA publishes to the tenant's own channels (WordPress site, Facebook Page) only after the tenant approves the piece (T11). It uses credentials the tenant supplies. The post appears under the tenant's name. The tenant can unpublish its own posts. |
| Built-in safeguards | (a) An automated **authenticity check** finds and removes customer reviews, star ratings, customer quotes, copy written as if by a customer ("I booked…", "my wife and I…") and claims of independence ("independent review", "not sponsored"). (b) A **daily publish limit** per tenant (default 20 per UTC day; AA can override it per tenant) to prevent spam. (c) Writer instructions that forbid inventing facts, statistics or testimonials. |
| Suspension tooling | An AA admin can **suspend** a tenant, which blocks its API and publishing at once, and **resume** it. An admin can also **offboard** a tenant, which revokes its key; this is reversible only by AA. |
| Records kept | Every model call: provider, model, stage, cost, time, tenant and job. AA's policy is not to put personal data in these records. Every publish and unpublish: piece, channel, external post id, status, and who acted and when. An admin audit log of tenant account actions. Retention: model-call records 180 days. Publish and audit records are currently kept with no end date; a retention window will be set before production. |
| TripPlanner (consumer site) | This is not a tenant product. Its AI disclosure is a permanent "AI-assisted" label in the site header and badges on AI-written narration. This clause does not cover it; it appears only in Q2. |

## 2. Draft clause

*Drafting note: written as a stand-alone schedule ("AI Schedule") so it can be attached to existing
tenant agreements by amendment and included in new ones. "Agreement", "Tenant", "Services", "Portal",
"Confidential Information" and "Personal Data" are assumed to be defined in the main agreement.
Counsel should align these terms.*

### Schedule [●]: Acceptable Use of AI Features

**[●].1 Definitions.** In this Schedule:

- **"AI Features"** means the parts of the Services that use artificial-intelligence or
  machine-learning models to generate, rewrite, translate, summarise, score or review content,
  including tour rewrites and social, blog and marketing content.
- **"AI Output"** means any content produced, in whole or in part, by the AI Features, including
  after editing by the Tenant.
- **"Model Provider"** means any third party whose AI model is used to provide the AI Features,
  including Anthropic, PBC (through Amazon Web Services) and any provider AA names in the
  [sub-processor list / Portal] from time to time.
- **"Model Provider Policies"** means the acceptable-use and usage policies of the Model Providers
  and of the cloud platform through which they are accessed, as updated from time to time,
  including the Anthropic Usage Policy (currently at https://www.anthropic.com/legal/aup) and the
  AWS Acceptable Use Policy.
- **"Channel"** means any website, social-media page or account, newsletter, marketplace or other
  medium on which the Tenant, or AA acting on the Tenant's instruction, publishes AI Output.
- **"Published Content"** means AI Output that has been published, posted, sent or otherwise made
  available to anyone outside the Tenant's organisation, by the Tenant or by AA on the Tenant's
  instruction.

**[●].2 Compliance with Model Provider Policies.**
(a) The Tenant must use the AI Features and AI Output only in compliance with this Schedule, the
Model Provider Policies and applicable law.
(b) The Tenant acknowledges that the Model Provider Policies apply to the Tenant as an end user of a
service that integrates the Model Providers' models, and that they may change. AA will use reasonable
efforts to notify the Tenant of material changes that affect the Tenant's use. A change binds the
Tenant from the later of (i) the date the Model Provider makes it effective and (ii) [●] days after
AA's notice. [*Drafting note: Anthropic's update takes effect 12/11/2026 regardless of AA's notice
date, so counsel must decide whether AA can rely on (i) alone; see Q5.*]
(c) If this Schedule and a Model Provider Policy conflict, the stricter rule applies.

**[●].3 Prohibited uses.** The Tenant must not use the AI Features or AI Output, and must not
publish Published Content, to:

1. **create fake reviews or testimonials:** customer reviews, ratings, endorsements, quotes or
   "guest stories" that were not given by a real customer about a real experience, or that are
   presented as independent when they are not;
2. **impersonate or invent people or organisations:** fake personas, sockpuppet accounts,
   content attributed to a named real person who did not write or approve it, or content that
   claims to come from a customer, journalist, guide or other third party when it does not;
3. **run deceptive or inauthentic campaigns:** astroturfing; networks of websites, outlets or
   accounts that appear independent but share a common source; content that misrepresents how
   widely a view is held; or bots that claim to be human;
4. **conceal sponsorship or commercial relationships:** presenting advertising, sponsored or
   commissioned content as independent or editorial, or removing a disclosure the law or a Channel
   requires;
5. **manipulate search engines or AI systems:** seeding the sources they draw on with content that
   misrepresents its origin, authorship or independence;
6. **send spam:** unsolicited bulk messages, or high-volume or repetitive posting that Channel
   rules or applicable law forbid, or that is designed to get around AA's publishing limits;
7. **publish false or misleading information:** in particular about entry, visa, health,
   safety or legal requirements, prices, availability, inclusions or the Tenant's licences and
   memberships;
8. **give high-risk advice:** use AI Output as advice on insurance (including travel-insurance
   underwriting or claims), finance, credit or tax, immigration or visas, legal, medical or other
   matters the Model Provider Policies classify as high-risk, unless a qualified person has
   meaningfully reviewed it with authority to change it before it is delivered, **and** the
   recipient is clearly told that AI was used;
9. **misuse personal data:** submit personal data to the AI Features except as the Agreement
   permits, or use the AI Features to compile or enrich profiles of individuals without their
   knowledge and agreement;
10. **copy without permission:** publish AI Output that reproduces third-party material without
    permission, or present AI-assisted work in a context that requires disclosure of AI use without
    making that disclosure; or
11. **circumvent safeguards:** try to bypass, disable or reverse-engineer AA's content checks,
    publishing limits or other safeguards, or use the AI Features for any purpose a Model Provider
    Policy prohibits.

**[●].4 AI-generated content: notice and disclosure.**
(a) *AA's notice.* AA will tell the Tenant, in the Portal, wherever it shows AI Output for review,
publication or export, that the content was generated with AI. The Tenant acknowledges that AI Output
can be inaccurate, incomplete or unsuitable, even after AA's automated checks.
(b) *Tenant's disclosure duty.* If applicable law, a Channel's rules or a regulator requires content
to be labelled as AI-generated or AI-assisted, the Tenant is responsible for adding that label to its
Published Content. [*Drafting note: AA's current position, still to be confirmed, is that marketing
copy published by a real business under its own name needs no AI label under the Anthropic policy
(Q1). Local law may require one (Q3). This sub-clause puts that duty on the Tenant either way.*]
(c) *Direct AI interaction.* If the Tenant uses the AI Features to communicate directly with its
customers or the public (for example a chat assistant or automated replies), the Tenant must tell
them they are interacting with AI rather than a human, at least at the start of each conversation or
permanently in the interface. The disclosure need not name AA or any Model Provider.
(d) The Tenant must not remove, hide or contradict any AI disclosure that AA displays to end users
through the Services.

**[●].5 Tenant responsibility for publication.**
(a) The Tenant is the publisher of all Published Content and is solely responsible for it,
including its accuracy, its compliance with applicable consumer-protection, advertising,
fair-trading and AI-labelling laws, and its compliance with each Channel's terms.
(b) The Tenant must review each item of AI Output before it is published. AA publishes to a Channel
only after the Tenant approves the item in the Portal (or through the API). The Tenant's approval
is its instruction to publish and its confirmation that it has reviewed the item.
(c) The Tenant is responsible for the Channel accounts and credentials it connects to the Services,
and for keeping its authorisations to publish on those Channels in force.
(d) AA's content checks, limits and notices help the Tenant comply. They do not move responsibility
for Published Content to AA, and AA has no obligation to monitor Published Content.
[*Drafting note: link (a) to (d) to the indemnity in the main agreement; see Q9.*]

**[●].6 Safeguards and limits.**
AA may, at any time and without liability:
(a) apply automated or human checks to AI Output, and remove, rewrite or hold any part that appears
to breach clause [●].3 (for example, a sentence that reads as a customer testimonial);
(b) set and change limits on the volume or frequency of generation and publication for each tenant
or each Channel; and
(c) decline to generate or publish an item that AA reasonably believes would breach this Schedule,
a Model Provider Policy or applicable law.

**[●].7 Suspension and termination.**
(a) AA may suspend the Tenant's access to the AI Features, to publishing, or to the Services as a
whole, with immediate effect and [with notice as soon as reasonably practicable / without prior
notice], if AA reasonably believes that:
(i) the Tenant has breached this Schedule or a Model Provider Policy;
(ii) the Tenant's use exposes AA or a Model Provider to legal, regulatory or reputational harm; or
(iii) a Model Provider, a Channel operator, a court or a competent authority requires it.
(b) Where the breach can be remedied and is not serious, AA will give the Tenant [●] days to remedy
it before suspending, unless (a)(iii) applies.
(c) AA may terminate the Agreement [or the AI Features] for a material breach of this Schedule, or
for repeated breaches, under clause [●] (Termination) of the Agreement. When the Tenant is offboarded,
its API keys are revoked.
(d) [*Optional, see Q7:*] While suspended, or on a Model Provider's or authority's request, AA may
unpublish from a connected Channel any Published Content that AA published on the Tenant's
instruction and that AA reasonably believes breaches clause [●].3. The Tenant authorises AA to use
the connected Channel credentials for that purpose.
(e) Suspension does not relieve the Tenant of its payment obligations or of responsibility for
Published Content already live.

**[●].8 Logging, records and cooperation.**
(a) The Tenant acknowledges that AA records, for each use of the AI Features: the model and provider
used, the processing stage, time, cost, and the resulting content; and for each publication or
unpublication: the item, Channel, external post identifier, status, the approving user and the time.
AA also keeps an audit log of administrative actions on the Tenant's account.
(b) AA uses these records to operate, secure and bill the Services; to detect and investigate abuse;
to demonstrate compliance with the Model Provider Policies; and to deal with disputes and
complaints.
(c) AA keeps these records for the periods in its [retention schedule / Data Processing Addendum].
[*Drafting note: current internal windows are 180 days for model-call records. Publish and audit
records are kept until a production window is set. Counsel should decide whether to state numbers
here; see Q8.*]
(d) AA may disclose relevant records to a Model Provider, a Channel operator, or a court or
competent authority where required by law, by the Model Provider Policies, or to investigate a
suspected breach of this Schedule. AA will [notify the Tenant where lawful].
(e) The Tenant must cooperate in good faith with any investigation by AA or a Model Provider into
suspected misuse, and must keep its own records of the Published Content it publishes outside the
Services.

**[●].9 Changes to this Schedule.** AA may update this Schedule to reflect changes in the Model
Provider Policies, applicable law, or the AI Features, by giving [●] days' notice through the Portal
or by email. A shorter period applies where a Model Provider or the law requires it. Continued use
of the AI Features after the effective date is acceptance.

**[●].10 No warranty on AI Output.** Except as expressly stated in the Agreement, AI Output is
provided "as is". AA does not warrant that AI Output is accurate, complete, original or fit for any
particular purpose. Clause [●] (Limitation of liability) of the Agreement applies.

## 6. Questions counsel must confirm

1. **Attribution of T2/T9 content (Q1).** Does content generated by AA's AI, approved by the tenant
   and published under the tenant's own name **without an AI label** comply with the policy's
   "origin or attribution" ban and its "plagiarize or submit AI-assisted work without proper …
   attribution" ban? AA's reading is yes, because the tenant is a real business that reviews and
   adopts the content as its own, and the policy does not require AI labels on marketing copy
   outside the high-risk categories. Does clause .4(b) need to say more?
2. **TripPlanner disclosure (Q2).** Is the permanent "AI-assisted" header label, together with the
   "AI narration" / "AA + AI" badges, enough AI disclosure for the public trip planner (no sign-in,
   no chat), given that the policy now accepts a disclosure "in the product interface"?
3. **Local AI-labelling and advertising law (Q3).** For the tenant markets [list: ●], do local laws
   require AI labels or disclosures stricter than the Anthropic policy? Examples to check: EU AI
   Act Article 50 transparency obligations and their application dates; EU unfair commercial
   practices rules on fake reviews; the US FTC rule on fake reviews and testimonials (16 CFR Part
   465); and Vietnam's rules on labelling AI-generated products. Should the Schedule name any of
   them?
4. **How the Schedule binds existing tenants (Q4).** Is a contract amendment needed, or can it be
   brought in through updated online terms accepted in the portal (click-through) or by continued
   use after notice? Is any tenant on a negotiated contract that needs individual sign-off?
5. **Timing (Q5).** The policy takes effect 12/11/2026. Can the Schedule take effect on that date
   for existing tenants, given the notice period in their current agreements? If not, what applies
   between 12/11 and the Schedule's effective date?
6. **Suspension without notice (Q6).** Is immediate suspension (clause .7(a)) enforceable under
   the governing law and the existing termination clause? Should there be a cure period, and how
   long?
7. **AA unpublishing through tenant credentials (Q7).** Should AA reserve the right to take down
   Published Content from a tenant's Channel using the stored credentials (optional clause .7(d)),
   or only suspend and ask the tenant to remove it?
8. **Logging and retention (Q8).** Are the records in clause .8 lawful and proportionate under
   the applicable data-protection law? Is AA a controller or a processor for each kind of record?
   Should retention periods appear in the Schedule or only in the DPA? May AA disclose records to
   a Model Provider (.8(d)) without first notifying the tenant?
9. **Liability allocation (Q9).** Should breaches of .3 and .5 fall under the existing indemnity,
   be uncapped, or have a separate cap? Does AA need a carve-out where an AA safeguard failed (for
   example, the authenticity check missed a testimonial)?
10. **Governing law and fit (Q10).** Which governing law and contract template applies to tenants
    (single template or per market)? Rewrite numbering and defined terms to match it.
11. **Naming Model Providers (Q11).** Should the Terms or a sub-processor list name the providers
    behind the AI Features? Today these are Anthropic (Claude, through AWS Bedrock), OpenAI (a
    reviewer/judge model), a decision-API provider (Jev) and Cohere (search embeddings). Does
    sending tenant content to them need the DPA or zero-data-retention terms noted in AA's ADR 0001?
12. **High-risk boundary (Q12).** Does generic travel content that mentions visas, entry
    requirements or travel insurance (for example "a visa on arrival is available") count as
    "advice" under the high-risk rules? Or does it only count when the content gives a personalised
    recommendation to an individual? This decides how strict clause .3(8) and the Booking
    boundary (§5) need to be.
13. **AA's own site (Q13, optional).** Should adventure.asia carry an "AI-assisted, reviewed by
    Adventure Asia" editorial note for AA-authored A-series content? (This is a brand decision;
    legally optional in AA's reading.)
