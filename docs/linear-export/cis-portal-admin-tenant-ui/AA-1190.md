## Problem

"My work" exists in CIS (content queues, AA-1156) and Booking (dashboard strip, AA-1159), but signing in does not tell each person what to do. S295 (27/11/2026) found: CIS admins land on the SRE Overview, not My work; about 13 kinds of approval are missing (3 in CIS, \~10 in Booking), which is most of the business owner's work; the price setup maker has no progress card; zero tiles take space (12 of 15 on Booking); and backlog items such as place cards inflate the "items waiting" total.

## Goal

In one session, all three staff roles (tech lead, business owner, price setup maker) land on My work in both apps and see their own next actions first.

## Scope

1. **1a, AA-CIS-App (M)**: admins and custom roles with `ops.read` land on `/admin/my-work`; the logo goes to the landing page; three approval tiles (Settings proposals, Compliance exceptions, Data erasure) and an Ops summary card for `ops.write`; Review Queue split into "ready" and "Held by policy"; place cards shown as backlog, not counted in the total; zero tiles collapse into one line.
2. **1b, AA-Booking (M)**: tiles for Price versions to approve, Add-ons to publish, Season and holiday proposals; a **Price setup progress** card from the price workbench; Trip checks split into assigned to you / unassigned; zero tiles collapse, tiles with work first; Sales overview collapsed for people without a sales focus; Inactive staff tile follows `staff.admin`; contract expiring label; remove the stale "catalog pages open in wave 2" line.
3. **1c, root (S)**: guides (business owner, price setup, CIS runbook) + `.vi.md` say "Start at My work".

Read-only counts from existing queries; no migration. Thresholds stay editable policy values, never hard-coded. Code never names people; roles come from capabilities and the business-owner mark.

Out of scope: shared kit component and `my_work.v2` contract (slices 2–3), remaining Booking approval tiles and "Yours waiting / Returned to you" (slices 4–5), focus sidebar, cross-app headline, onboarding cards, pins; trip case auto-assignment (separate issue when needed); AA-Partner.

## Done when

* A CIS admin E2E login lands on My work, and the three approval tiles show counts that match their target pages.
* The Booking dashboard shows the new tiles and the Price setup progress card with counts that match the price workbench.
* Zero tiles are collapsed into one line in both apps; place cards are not in the CIS total.
* Trip checks show "assigned to you" and "unassigned" separately.
* Playwright evidence at 1440 and 390, light and dark, with three E2E accounts (admin, content, contracting); nothing approved during the check.
