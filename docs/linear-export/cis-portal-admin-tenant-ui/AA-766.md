# AA-766 — [Admin] Tenant 360 panel — Cost, Quality and Markets tabs (margin vs plan, per-gate pass/held, research coverage) (G9)

## Problem

The Tenant 360 inline panel in `/admin/tenants` ([AA-666](https://linear.app/aa-ecosys/issue/AA-666/admin-tenant-360-full-tenant-management-page-profile-brand-content)) has no per-tenant cost, quality or market coverage view. Until G1 (PR [AdventureAsia365-Ecosys/AA-CIS-App#661](https://linear.app/aa-ecosys/review/fix-tenant-id-on-every-llm-call-via-a-job-bound-contextvar-aa-741-g1-4fa2755bb35c)) tenant LLM cost was not attributable at all; after it, `shared.llm_call_log.tenant_id` makes it possible. S229 plan `docs/architecture/t-series-audit-and-plan.md` §7 G9.

## Scope

1. Cost tab: $/stage and $/piece (blog vs social separately), $/tour, month-to-date vs plan revenue → margin, per-tenant budget.
2. Quality tab: pass/held per T10 gate, tenant edit rate, brand distinctness.
3. Markets tab: `segment_research_log` coverage per tenant market, `unmapped_market_requests` queue.
   Out of scope: a separate tenant page (it stays the inline panel).

## Done when

* On production admin, the panel for atlas-hearth shows non-zero cost by stage that matches a direct `llm_call_log` query for that tenant (±$0.01), and the Markets tab shows coverage for its US/UK markets; screenshots desktop + mobile, light + dark.
