## Problem

The tech lead asked (S265) for a more interactive TripPlanner, benchmarked on AI planners such as [Trip.com](<http://Trip.com>), Tripadvisor and others: activity icons, visual place images and better interaction. Benchmark and list: `docs/audits/2026-11-03-S265-ecosystem-restructure/` README §8.4 (items 1–7). It follows R5 (multi-tenant shell).

## Scope

The items of §8.4:

1. Photo per stop and day, from CIS photo coverage ([AA-997](https://linear.app/aa-ecosys/issue/AA-997/places-geocode-accuracy-coordinates-from-the-geocoder-only-route)).
2. Activity icons from the kit icon set.
3. Map and itinerary sync: hover or select highlights the stop.
4. Drag to reorder days and stops on desktop and mobile.
5. Planning chat constrained to AA live tours and places (budget capped per tenant; paid runs on a subset with a yes).
6. Share or print view.
7. Empty, error and loading states in the kit.

All of it is branded per tenant via the R5 theme tokens.

## Done-when

* On Dev, a synthetic tenant's planner shows photos and icons on every stop.
* Reorder works on 390 width.
* The chat answers only with AA tours (test set plus one live check).
* Screenshots at 1440/390, light and dark; Lighthouse performance is not below today's.
