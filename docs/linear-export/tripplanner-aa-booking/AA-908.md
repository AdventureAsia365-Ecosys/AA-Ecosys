## Problem

[AA-680](https://linear.app/aa-ecosys/issue/AA-680/aaa-identity-customer-otp-auth-shared-with-tripplanner-staff-rbac) holds two halves on different timelines (S244 master plan, verified). The staff half (Booking staff on Cognito WORKFORCE, RBAC) is live; the customer half has not started and belongs to ADR 0006 Phase 4 ([AA-703](https://linear.app/aa-ecosys/issue/AA-703/auth-identity-epic-adr-0006-cognito-workforce-external-pools-email) epic). Split out so [AA-680](https://linear.app/aa-ecosys/issue/AA-680/aaa-identity-customer-otp-auth-shared-with-tripplanner-staff-rbac) can close on the staff half.

## Scope

1. Traveller sign-in on the Cognito EXTERNAL pool: email one-time code first; Google / Apple / Facebook per ADR 0006 (S241 decision).
2. A TripPlanner guest trip (hashed guest token, tp-011/012) is attached to the account at first sign-in.
3. "My Trips" in TripPlanner (and the Booking customer view), plus the "My itineraries / share / PDF" leftovers of the canceled epic [AA-689](https://linear.app/aa-ecosys/issue/AA-689/epic-tripplanner-tripcom-style-planner-on-aa-data-jira-pr-11) if still wanted.
4. Shared with Booking leads (ADR 0004 O6: lead → customer on OTP).

## Done when

* A new traveller signs in with an email code on Dev, the guest trip shows under My Trips, and the Booking lead links to the same customer (screenshots + DB rows).
* WAF on the sign-up path (S241 decision: on when traveller sign-up opens).
