## Problem

The S283 UI audit (`docs/audits/2026-11-17-S283-fe-audit/README.md` section 6) found that the Booking season-calendar job reports success while every step times out (405/405), and several Booking admin pages show ids, wrong copy or missing Vietnamese. (B0, the test pricing rule dated 01/01/2027, was fixed in S283: Infra #899/#900.)

## Scope

1. **B1 Season calendars actually assigned** (S, top 6): rewrite `UNASSIGNED_SQL` to pick the batch from `booking.trip_plans` first, then look up tours one by one; mark the job degraded when a step is skipped; show it on the job view; CI guard. Thresholds on the Settings page.
2. **B2 Booking catalog and prices** (M), **B3 names, not ids** (S), **B4 Booking copy** (S, includes removing the B2C "Inquiries" tab copy), **B5 Booking Vietnamese** (L).
3. **D1 Dev data hygiene, Booking part** (writes, tech lead approval): test suppliers, test FX rows, seed notes, un-sell the test trip plan, 2026–2027 holidays.
4. Optional hardening from the B0 review: tie-break `approved_at DESC` in `RULE_SQL` / `FX_SQL`, and a warning on `/pricing/rules` when a scheduled row lowers margin, floor or RRP.

Out of scope: the B2C table drop ([AA-1061](https://linear.app/aa-ecosys/issue/AA-1061/booking-r4c-b2c-table-drop-next-free-bk-key-apply-from-2026-11-28)).
