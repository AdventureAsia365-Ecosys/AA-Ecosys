## Problem

S254 audit of all 9,545 CIS place names (`docs/audits/2026-10-28-S254-geocode-audit/`): the 7,924 resolved names are about **75% correct** (95% CI 71–80%). Geoapify-placed points are \~91% correct, LLM-proposed coordinates only \~55–60% (3,770 rows, \~1,400 of \~1,700 estimated bad pins). Error types: 5–100 km off in the right region, town centroid instead of the site, non-places pinned, same-name place in the wrong city (Xi'an sights pinned in Beijing), HK/MO stored as CN. 1,621 names are unresolved (1,203 pending, 418 unknown) across 14 causes (small unknown places 519, descriptive phrases 277, route legs 223, spelling variants 102, other-country places 94, generic names 90, non-places 81, …).

## Scope (system fixes; no hand patches)

1. Coordinates only from a geocoder: the LLM gives name + country + town; Geoapify gives the point, accepted only within a set distance of the day ±1 stops. Re-locate the 3,770 LLM-placed rows (check the free-tier quota first, run in subsets).
2. Route-coherence guard (distance to day ±1 stops) → far pins to the /admin/places review queue (replaces `tour_spread`).
3. Generic landmark disambiguation by the town in the same sentence/day (never the overnight hotel) + named-landmark allow-list.
4. Pre-filter before paid calls: split route legs and "A and B", strip wrappers / altitude / "(or similar)", new `non_place` status.
5. Explicit centroid fallback (area precision + `review_reason=centroid_fallback`).
6. Small fixes: country by point-in-polygon (HK/MO), lenient country reader ("Tibet, China"), keep a leading "An" in proper nouns, coastal tolerance, backfill provider/precision on the 652 rows without a geocoder, stop retrying after the first null.
7. Content (separate): hand pins for the verified wrong rows, alias table.
8. Re-measure with a fresh random sample split by provenance.

## Done when

* A fresh blind sample (≥ 200 resolved names, split by provenance) is ≥ 90% correct.
* Unresolved names reduced by the pre-filter (non-places marked, legs split) with counts before/after.
* Geocoder spend stays within the free tier (or the cost is stated before running).
