## Problem

Jira PR-30: hotel data today is synthetic and typed by hand; one hotel can belong to only one supplier, seasons are free text, there is no dedup, no meal-plan model, no allotment and no re-confirm reminders. AA's contract is with the DMC, not the hotel.

## Scope (T8–T12, B7, B8)

* T8: property master (one hotel, many contracts from many DMCs) with dedup by normalised name + place + coordinates; price guard and service-booking supplier taken from the contract; room-type aliases.
* T9: board basis RO/BB/HB/FB/AI, BB default, tour or quote may pick HB/FB (AI for resorts); warn on a double dinner when a meal service exists that day.
* T10: no GIATA; keep external_codes (DMC code, room for GIATA later); Excel templates for facts and room types; DMC price-sheet importer with business checks (HB cheaper than BB, season gaps, +60 % jumps), diff before approval; season and per-night rate tables.
* T11: contracting enters facts and prices in Booking from the DMC sheet with source and confirmation date; content writes descriptions and picks images in CIS; one hotel id; CIS reads facts only; re-confirm reminder; hotel confirmation number on bookings; tour wording never hard-codes a hotel name.
* B7: all prices net; "published + commission %" converted to net with both source numbers kept.
* B8: allotment / free sale / on request per AA–DMC contract, room type and dates in the shared restriction table; on request by default.

## Done-when

* One hotel with 2 DMC contracts; the second DMC's "70 USD, 20 %" sheet imports as 56 USD net with both source numbers kept; the booking goes to the DMC of the price used.
* A 3-night HB quote for 2 adults and a 7-year-old matches the cost; allotment of 2 rooms with 7 release days makes the third room "on request".
* CI green, deployed, live check recorded here.
