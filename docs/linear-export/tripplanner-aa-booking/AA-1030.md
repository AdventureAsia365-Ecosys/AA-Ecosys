## Problem

B2B pricing has one global `b2b` margin rule: no per-agency tier, rate card or contract price list; the CRM agency types its retail price by hand with no default markup (research `docs/audits/2026-11-01-S263-pricing-and-plans/README.md` §1, gap 3). Tech lead (S263): build the mechanism now; the numbers (margins per channel, tier levels) are the business owner's call and get asked through Jira.

## Scope

* `b2b_partners` tier (e.g. Standard / Silver / Gold) with a margin rule per tier (maker-checker like `pricing_rules`), optional per-partner contract rate card overriding the tier, valid_from/valid_to.
* B2B quotes and the CRM's AA options use the partner's tier/card price.
* CRM: agency default retail markup % (per agency, per product type) suggested on new options; below-AA-"from" warning kept (ADR 0005a).
* Admin pages in Booking for tiers and partner assignment (kit UI).
* Question list for the business owner (margins per channel, tier levels) prepared for the session-end Jira draft.

## Done-when

* Two invented partners on different tiers get different B2B prices for the same E2E tour live; CRM option shows the suggested retail price from the agency markup; screenshots; $0.
