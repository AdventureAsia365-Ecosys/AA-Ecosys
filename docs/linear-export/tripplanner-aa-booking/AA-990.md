### 2026-10-27 09:32 — nghiep pham quoc

S253: ranged day headings fixed end to end.

**Root cause:**
- The writer v2 emitted `"day": "12-15"`.
- validate's AA-228 dash normaliser turned that heading into "Day 12 — 15 —".
- The canonical readers (itinerary_utils, `catalog._tour_day_text`, places/validity) knew only "Day N —", so days 13–15 vanished.
- The day gap check also counted the source range as one day, so nothing flagged it.

**Fix:**
- CIS #1061 (+ restore #1064) deployed, api :860 / worker :426, `f8545b38`.
- One rule in `day_parse` ("Day A-B" covers A..B when A < B ≤ A+14) for every reader. The normaliser keeps valid ranges, only at the start of a line.
- The day gap check and `final_day_guard` count trusted source ranges.
- The writer may keep a range when the source groups days and never invents per-day content.
- **CIS migration 289 applied** (`/health/ready` schema 289): `catalog._tour_day_text` gives one row per covered day, and `v_tour.duration_days` uses the highest covered day.
- Review found 3 medium issues, all fixed.

**Data:**
- Live scan of all 892 tours: 9 tours were short of `duration_days`.
- S1 rewrite of all 9 (Sonnet 5.5): all publishable, score 10, no failure codes.
- **Sri Lanka `b45db4c8` now covers 16/16 days** (ranges 2-3 … 12-15).
- 8 tours still cover fewer days than `duration_days`. Most source itineraries have fewer day markers than the stated duration (e.g. Nepal Tibet Tour 19 markers for 20 days, Annapurna 13 for 14, Ride the Trail 12 for 14). The writer never invents days, so this is source data, and the day oracle reports it as `SOURCE_DAY_CONTRADICTION`. `256f07bd` (9 source markers, 8 covered) needs a closer look.
