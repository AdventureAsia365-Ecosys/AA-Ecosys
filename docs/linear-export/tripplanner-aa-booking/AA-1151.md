## Problem

Jira PR-29: customers and agencies need optional activities per place and day, sold only with an AA supplier. Today activities are loose services without places, sale rules or content workflow.

## Scope (B1, B3, B4, T7)

* B1: AA content staff create and edit the activity and its content (CIS); contracting enters contract and net price (Booking); a second person approves before sale.
* Place links so activities match a day's sleep place and places visited.
* B3: a day without an approved, in-force price hides the activity for that day only; admin sees "contract expiring" 60 days ahead (one expiry job for every supplier contract).
* B4: child price per the [AA-1145](https://linear.app/aa-ecosys/issue/AA-1145/booking-one-supply-model-pax-types-structured-contract-terms) engine.
* T7: on request by default; cut-off in hours; operating weekdays and departure times; minimum pax; stop-sale and "free sale up to N pax per booking" as rows of the shared restriction table ([AA-1145](https://linear.app/aa-ecosys/issue/AA-1145/booking-one-supply-model-pax-types-structured-contract-terms)); daily allotment when a supplier gives seats.
* Read model for TripPlanner/CRM (view of place add-ons with in-force prices only).

## Done-when

* Importing a file of 5 activities creates 5 drafts; a second person publishes.
* The place add-on view returns only days with an in-force price; child price follows B4; the 60-day alert shows in admin.
* CI green, deployed, live check recorded here.
