## Problem

Nghiệp (S252, 27/10/2026): the customer-facing pages should look friendlier, more professional and more polished, and may use motion. For example, the original [adventure.asia](<http://adventure.asia>) site has a banner that scrolls sideways. Today TripPlanner (B2C, `aa-tripplanner.vercel.app`) is functional but plain.

## Scope

1. **Research.** Capture and analyse [adventure.asia](<http://adventure.asia>):
   * hero and horizontal-moving banner, layout, typography, colour, imagery, motion patterns, tone of copy;
   * the current TripPlanner pages: home, destinations, tour page, plan.
2. **Design.** 2–3 independent directions, judged and one picked. They keep the Adventure Asia brand, use the shared design tokens from [AA-994](https://linear.app/aa-ecosys/issue/AA-994/ui-one-design-system-for-the-staff-and-partner-apps-kit-coverage), and set a motion language: hero carousel or marquee, scroll reveals, card hover, page transitions. Everything respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
3. **Build** the chosen direction on home, destinations and tour pages. The planner gets a light touch that keeps its flows.
4. **Quality bars:**
   * Lighthouse mobile performance ≥ 85 and LCP ≤ 2.5 s on the tour page;
   * CLS ≤ 0.05;
   * no layout shift from the carousel; images sized and lazy;
   * keyboard and screen-reader friendly carousel (pause and labels);
   * 390 and 1440, light theme first;
   * no page-level horizontal overflow.

## Done when

* Production pages are live in the new design, with before/after screenshots at 1440 and 390 plus a short screen recording of the motion.
* The Lighthouse/CWV numbers above are met on the production tour and home pages; reduced-motion is verified.
* 0 console errors, and the [AA-994](https://linear.app/aa-ecosys/issue/AA-994/ui-one-design-system-for-the-staff-and-partner-apps-kit-coverage) visual job is green for TripPlanner pages.

Related: [AA-994](https://linear.app/aa-ecosys/issue/AA-994/ui-one-design-system-for-the-staff-and-partner-apps-kit-coverage) (shared tokens), [AA-990](https://linear.app/aa-ecosys/issue/AA-990/s1-propagation-trip-building-and-readers-on-s1-v2-data-tripplanner) (TripPlanner on S1 v2 data).
