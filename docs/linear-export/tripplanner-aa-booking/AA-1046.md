## Problem

[AA-1037](https://linear.app/aa-ecosys/issue/AA-1037/booking-r4-booking-b2b-only-drop-the-b2c-side-every-invoice-issued) (R4) closed in S270 with two scope parts left on purpose:

1. The frozen B2C tables are still in Booking. The S270 DRY RUN (root #571) has the report, a read-only counting script, and a draft destructive migration that is not applied.
2. The VAT e-invoice number (Decree 70/2025) is typed in by the accountant (`e_invoice_no`, phase 1). The provider API was planned as about 1 session.

The Booking `CHECK channel = 'b2b'` waits for the drop. Added now, it would refuse the DM-6.7 erasure redaction of old b2c rows.

## Scope

* **B2C drop:**
  * Run the draft migration from root #571 through the destructive-ops procedure: DRY RUN, then tech-lead approval, then apply.
  * Archive the four customer money tables into `booking.retired_b2c_record`, with retain_until set to the year of the last entry + 10 years.
  * Keep the retained rows: cases, supplier bills and payouts, files, audit, notifications.
  * Then add the `channel = 'b2b'` CHECK.
* **TripPlanner My Payments** (`booking.customer.v1`): retire it with the drop.
* **E-invoice provider API:** issue the VAT e-invoice from Booking through the chosen provider, and store its number and status. The provider choice is the business owner's call.

## Done-when

* On Dev, the B2C tables are dropped after an approved DRY RUN. The archive table holds every money row, its copy checksum matches, and nothing in CIS, Booking, TripPlanner or the CRM reads a dropped table.
* An agency invoice gets its e-invoice number from the provider sandbox, with no manual step.
* Tests green; CONTEXT.md and data standards updated.

Created in S270 with the tech lead's approval (05/11).
