## Problem

Jira PR-29 front end: customers on TripPlanner and agencies in the CRM must see and pick optional activities per day. Agency pages show no prices today and agency prices end in odd cents.

## Scope (T6, B2)

* T6: each day card lists activities matched by sleep place first, then places visited.
* Customer ticks travel with the lead (sv: codes); agencies quote them in the CRM through an "AA add-on" drawer.
* B2 (option C): the agency page shows "from X per person" for tours and activities = AA B2B price × the agency's own markup (RRP if none), rounded up per a currency step chosen by the agency (default USD 1, VND 10,000), applied to every suggested price in the CRM; a switch lets the agency hide prices; the B2B price is never exposed.

## Done-when

* The HCMC day card shows at least one add-on; a ticked add-on reaches the lead with its sv: code; the agency quotes it in the CRM.
* An agency page shows a rounded "from" price per its markup and hides it when switched off.
* CI green, deployed, screenshots, live check recorded here.
