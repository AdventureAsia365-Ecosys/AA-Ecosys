## What Asian Trails runs (public sources, first pass)

* **eConnect**: B2B agent portal launched at WTM London, end of 2022. Agents search and book hotels, transfers, tours/excursions, multi-day tours and flights with real-time rates and availability, map view, and multi-country trip building.
* **API connectivity** for OTAs and tour operators, direct or via aggregators (TravelgateX, Juniper). Covers hotels, transfers and tours.
* **Lemax** cloud DMC reservation system as back office (contracted 2018), integrated with accounting and the website. **Cloud Tariff**: online rate sheets for partners.

Sources: [https://www.asiantrails.travel/?p=1032376](<https://www.asiantrails.travel/?p=1032376>) · [https://lemax.net/news/lemax-unrivaled-on-the-competitive-tender-for-the-digital-transformation-of-asian-trails/](<https://lemax.net/news/lemax-unrivaled-on-the-competitive-tender-for-the-digital-transformation-of-asian-trails/>) · [https://asiantrails.travel/](<https://asiantrails.travel/>)
