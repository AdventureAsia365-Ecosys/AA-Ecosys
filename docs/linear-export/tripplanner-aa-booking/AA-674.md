# AA-674 — [TripPlanner] Multi-tour trip composition + route-constrained "suggest next" + filters (country, activity, intensity, duration)

## Rule (Nghiệp, 28/09)

A trip may combine **several tours**. Every pinned place must be covered by a chain of **legs** (day-spans of real AA tours), linked through **junctions** (see the Tour Graph issue). Places that would make the trip uncoverable are not offered. Goal: a complete Trip Case ready for booking.

## Scope

* **Coverage check (deterministic)** for the current pins:
  * find the minimal chain of legs that covers them, preferring fewer tours, fewer transfers and fewer extra days;
  * return the chain, total days, tours used, and the transfers between legs.
* **Suggest next:**
  * candidates = graph neighbours of the last stop (continuing the current leg) plus junction exits to other tours;
  * keep only candidates for which the coverage check still passes;
  * rank by the existing taste (pgvector) + distance, plus a small bonus for staying in the same tour (fewer transfers).
  * No LLM. Cacheable per `(pins hash)`.
* **Filters** (Browse Lambda, indexed WHERE, `backend/browse/filters.py` pattern): country, activity category (8), intensity, duration. They apply to both map tiles and suggestions.
* **UI:**
  * greyed / hidden places with the reason "no AA tour covers this combination";
  * a trip panel showing legs with the source tour name and days, plus transfer notes.
* **Jev (optional):** a Score "fits the traveller's stated intent" to re-rank among feasible candidates only. Measure cost vs the gain first.

## Done when

On Dev data (after the re-extraction), 20 random pin sets are either all coverable with a shown leg chain or blocked with a reason, and suggest-next never returns an uncoverable place (property test).

## Requirements from Jira PR-11 (Ms. Thư, notes of 26/09) — build to these

PR-11 asks for a visual map planner similar to [Trip.com Trip.Planner](<https://www.trip.com/webapp/tripmap/tripplanner?source=seo_H5_homepage>), using AA data. The 26/09 notes:

1. **Recommend trip routes that exist in AA data.** Suggest-next per pin (above) is not enough on its own. The planner should also propose **whole routes**: leg chains of real AA tours ([AA-673](https://linear.app/aa-ecosys/issue/AA-673/tripplanner-tour-graph-read-model-destinations-within-tour-day) legs + junctions) that fit the traveller's destination and duration, drawn on the map as day-by-day polylines. The traveller picks one as the starting trip, then edits it. Each proposal shows its source tour(s) and total days.
2. **Activity → selection.** At each stop/day the traveller **selects or deselects individual activities**, taken from the real AA tour days at that destination (`itinerary_components` activity, intensity, duration hint), not only a category filter. Selections are trip events, kept in the draft, and passed to the advisor. The coverage check must still hold, and an activity no AA tour offers there is not selectable.
3. **Extra day → add.** The traveller can **add days**: stay an extra night at a stop, a free day, or extend the end. Total days and the day-by-day panel update. An added day is marked as a **customization on top of the AA leg** (it is not part of any tour), so the Trip Case / advisor sees exactly what differs from the source tours. Removing an added day is also possible.

**Reference UX ([Trip.com](<http://Trip.com>) entry screen, checked 29/09):** start form with *Starting from*, *Heading to* (country / city / landmark), *Date / Duration*, *Preferences*; two entry modes, **Create it myself** and **Plan a trip with AI**; *My itineraries*; map on the right. Deeper screens need a [Trip.com](<http://Trip.com>) sign-in and could not be inspected.

**Done when (additions):**

* For a destination and duration on Dev data, the planner offers at least one AA route proposal drawn on the map, and picking it fills the day panel.
* Activities can be selected per stop, and the selection survives reload (trip events) and reaches the advisor draft.
* Adding and removing an extra day updates totals, keeps coverage valid, and shows the day as a customization in the draft.

### 2026-09-29 06:53 — nghiep pham quoc

Step 2 done: route-constrained "suggest next" (TripPlanner #58, merged + deployed, 29/09).

**Candidates are what real tours do next from the trip's last stop:**
- take every Tour Graph `tour_stop` within 150 km of the last stop (the same place, or a transfer to another tour);
- for each such tour, the candidates are the components of the days after that stop, up to the tour's next stop elsewhere.

**Other changes**
- The ranking is unchanged (taste + distance). When no tour passes near the last stop, it falls back to taste within the trip's countries.
- The response carries `mode` (`route` | `taste`); each suggestion carries `tour_count` and a `why` ("Next on N Adventure Asia tours from your last stop (a X km transfer)").
- Fix: `_current_components` did not select `destination_id`, so pinned destinations were never excluded.
