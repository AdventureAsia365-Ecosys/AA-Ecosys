## Problem

The business owner prices trips per person on a double-room basis by group size (Jira PR-31 example, valid 20/01–31/03/2027: 2 pax USD 2190, 4 pax 1770, 5–7 pax 1745, 8 pax 1550; bands wanted: 1, 2, 4, 5–7, 8–10). Booking computes tour prices on fixed bands hard-coded in `backend/app/catalog/cost_sheet.py` (`PAX_BANDS` = 1, 2, 3–4, 5–6, 7–10, 11+). That breaks the rule that every policy value is editable in admin, and the bands do not match how the business prices.

Hand price versions (Excel / price versions) already accept any Pax min / Pax max; seasons, twin-share per person, single supplement and multi-supplier components already exist.

## Scope

* A versioned pricing policy parameter for the group-size bands (Pricing › Policy, second person or flagged business-owner self-approval, like the other parameters), seeded with the business owner's bands.
* The cost sheet, the computed price matrix, the readiness view, the workbook export/import and the quote calculator read the bands from the policy in force on the price date; existing computed rows are recomputed on the next recompute.
* Validation: bands are contiguous from 1, no overlap, last band open or capped, at most 8 bands.
* Docs: Booking CONTEXT.md, the business-owner pricing runbook (GUIDE-12).

## Done when

* Pricing › Policy shows the bands with their guide; a change needs approval and is audited.
* A recompute produces matrix rows on the new bands; the quote for 4, 6 and 9 pax picks the right band.
* Tests cover validation and band lookup; CI green; deployed; live check recorded here.
