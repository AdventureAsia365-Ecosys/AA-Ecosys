## Problem

The S285 live check of [AA-1139](https://linear.app/aa-ecosys/issue/AA-1139/add-ons-tripplanner-module-in-the-agency-app-site-link-lead-channel) found three gaps in the agency's planner:

1. **Leads can be lost.** A tenant with the `crm_core` entitlement but no CRM agency yet shows effective lead channel CRM. The CRM lead pull then logs `skipped_agency` for its leads. Those leads reach neither the CRM nor the fallback e-mail/webhook route.
2. **Locale.** With locale `vi`, the planner sets `html lang="vi"`, but its own copy stays English.
3. **Theme.** The "TRIP PLANNER" label and the dashed "Start building" box keep the default orange under a tenant theme. The rest follows the agency's accent colours.

## Scope

1. The effective lead channel is CRM only when the tenant has a live CRM agency. A CRM agency is checked through the CIS contract (tenant.addons.v1 / the CIS 390 view), never by reading CRM tables. Otherwise the agency's fallback route applies (e-mail, webhook or none). Leads already marked `skipped_agency` are re-delivered once through the fallback route, if one is set.
2. The planner's UI copy is translated (EN + VI) and follows the tenant locale.
3. The remaining fixed-colour elements use the tenant theme tokens.

## Done when

* A synthetic tenant with `crm_core` and no CRM agency submits a lead. It is delivered through its e-mail or webhook route, and the CRM pull no longer skips it. Shown live, with the delivery status in the agency app's Trip planner leads list.
* With locale `vi`, the planner shows Vietnamese copy (screenshots 1440 and 390).
* Under a tenant theme, no default-orange element remains (screenshots).
