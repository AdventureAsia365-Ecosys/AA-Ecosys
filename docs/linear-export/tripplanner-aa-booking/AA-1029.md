## Problem

AA-Booking owns pricing (ADR 0005: supplier contracts/rates, margin rules per channel, FX, `trip_plan_prices` matrix, B2B quotes) but nothing links it to the published catalogue (research `docs/audits/2026-11-01-S263-pricing-and-plans/README.md` §1): published tours have no `trip_plans` row, the price matrix is typed by hand (cost sheet from components not automated), `booking.v_from_prices` (`price.from.v1`) is not built, TripPlanner shows no price, the portal shows the parsed `price_raw` string, price parity uses `price_hint`. Tech lead (S263): build everything now so that loading supplier data makes prices flow at once.

## Scope

* Bridge: every published master tour gets a Booking trip plan (sync job/contract, idempotent), with `price_raw` kept as a reviewed reference only.
* Cost sheet: compute net per season × pax band × validity from the plan's components and the rates in force; fill `trip_plan_prices` per channel through the existing engine; recompute on rate/rule/FX change; hand overrides kept (`overridden`).
* `booking.v_from_prices` contract view `price.from.v1`; TripPlanner tour page and the portal marketplace read it (fallback: no price shown); price parity reads the matrix.
* Supplier rate-sheet Excel import checked end to end with an invented fixture sheet.
* Price history: dated rows instead of replace-the-set only.

## Done-when

* Loading one invented supplier rate sheet + one margin rule on Dev produces prices for an E2E tour in Booking, the "from" price on TripPlanner and in the portal (screenshots), with no hand step; $0.
