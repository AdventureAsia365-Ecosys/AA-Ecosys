**S199 (28/09) — root cause of the DFS balance drain (P0 bug)**

**Where it comes from:**
- `api/routers/v1_tours.py::_run_research_only()` fires `run_segment_research()` after **every tenant T2 rewrite**.
- `services/acp_contract/segment_research.py::run_segment_research()` selects `canonical_place, canonical_action FROM acp_contract.atom_segment` with **no filter**.
- Since AA-545 made Segments platform-wide, one tenant rewrite researches every stale place on the platform (2,222 places) × the tenant's markets. The function's docstring ("the tenant's current Segments") is stale.

**What it cost** (`shared.dfs_call_log`, 25/09): 1,169 live calls, **$49.02**, in two bursts at 05:xx and 08:xx UTC (AU/US/UK).

| Endpoint | USD |
|---|---|
| `search_volume_bulk` | 39.24 |
| `keywords_for_keywords` | 9.00 |
| `serp_advanced` | 0.84 |

On top of that, ~7.9k Haiku `a3_search_demand` calls ($10.27 over 30 days). There is **no scheduler** involved.

**Proposed fix** (new issue, pending Nghiệp's approval):
1. Scope research to the places of the tour just rewritten, or move it entirely to an admin-triggered research job.
2. Add a DB-configured DFS budget per run and per day, with a hard stop.
3. Show a pre-run cost estimate in Cost Operations.
4. Add a Jev relevance gate before buying (AA-643).
