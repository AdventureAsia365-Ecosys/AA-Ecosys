## Goal

Upgrade S4.2 from the **v1** port (formula-direct) to Ms. Thư's **v2** redesign (`stage4.2 .../CONTEXT_v2.md` + `SKILL_v2.md`): a 9-business-goal strategist. This is the largest fidelity gap vs the research spec and is NOT covered by the harness audit.

## Why

v1 (shipped [AA-93](https://linear.app/aa-ecosys/issue/AA-93/acp-s42-social-media-content-engine-dual-mode-8-channels-11-formulas)) exposes "11 formulas" directly — wrong abstraction for non-tech B2B operators. v2 hides formulas and drives content from a business goal.

## v2 scope (delta over v1)

1. **9-goal selection workflow**: human picks 1 of {Promotion, Lead-gen, Conversion, Awareness, Trust, Engagement, Event, Explanation, Partner}; agent maps goal→formula **internally**.
2. **Goal→formula mapping** (internal): Promotion→AIDA · Lead-gen→AIDA/PAS · Conversion→AIDA/SLAP · Awareness→Hook-Insight-CTA/5W1H · Trust→Problem-Insight-Proof-Action/FAB · Engagement→Hook-Value-CTA/BAB · Event→5W1H+AIDA · Explanation→FAB · Partner→Problem-Insight-Proof-Action.
3. **CTA as a separate step** after goal, before angles.
4. **Hide formula names** by default; show business reason + final style.
5. Content-seed handling + **Quality Editor Pass** (revises, not just reports).
6. Reconcile formula set: research reference library = 14 frameworks; ACP says 11 — pick canonical N.
