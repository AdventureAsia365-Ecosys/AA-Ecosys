## Problem

A connection's monthly budget lives in three places that must be kept in sum by hand: the provider budget (CIS setting `cis.spend.connection_provider_budget_usd`, [AA-1113](https://linear.app/aa-ecosys/issue/AA-1113/ai-gateway-pii-scrub-before-every-free-text-ai-call-and-one-hard-cap)), the CIS share (the connection's Monthly budget in AI gateway › Connections) and the CRM share (CRM setting `crm.ai.connection_monthly_cap_usd`). TripPlanner has no share at all (it is counted inside CIS) and Booking has none. On 19/11 the tech lead had to give the CRM 5 USD on acc1 and then lower the CIS share from 100 to 95 by hand on another page. Tech lead decision G3 (19/11, S285 decision page): budgets become rows `(app, connection_key)`; the S285 gateway advisory (`docs/audits/2026-11-19-S285-session/advisory-gateway.md`) describes it.

## Scope

1. **One source of truth per connection**: the provider budget (total USD per month) and one row per app (`cis`, `tripplanner`, `crm`, `booking`, the list from `shared.llm_app_profile`) with a fixed USD share; CIS gets the **remainder** automatically (total minus the other apps' rows), so adding a share for one app never needs a manual edit elsewhere.
2. **One screen**: AI gateway › Budgets shows each connection with its total, every app's share, spend month to date per app, and the remainder; edit the total or any app's share in place (reason ≥ 20 characters, audited, versioned like Settings). The Connections drawer shows the same numbers read-only and links to it.
3. **The apps read their own row without cross-app grants**: CIS from its table; the CRM through the existing CRM settings relay (`crm.ops.v1 /settings`) or a CIS-owned contract view it already may read (no new grant on CRM data; ADR 0008a); TripPlanner through the control plane it already reads (ADR 0009 d13).
4. **Migration path**: expand (new rows seeded from today's three values: acc1 total 100, CRM 5, CIS remainder 95; acc2 25 + CRM 5; anthropic-direct 150), dual-read for one release, then retire `crm.ai.connection_monthly_cap_usd` and the hard-coded `cis_share_usd` / `crm_share_usd` columns of the split API (contract).
5. **Rule kept**: enforcement before every call, per app per connection (dev hard stop, prod alert + failover per the S283 policy).

## Done when

* On Dev, the tech lead changes only the CRM row on acc1 (e.g. 5 → 8) on one screen and the CIS remainder follows (92) with no other edit; the CRM gateway enforces 8 and CIS enforces 92 (proved by a $0 probe or the ledger).
* TripPlanner has its own row and is enforced.
* The old CRM setting is retired after the dual-read release; Budgets shows no "shares exceed provider budget" warning for any connection.
