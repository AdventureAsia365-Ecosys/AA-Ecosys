## Problem

[AA-741](https://linear.app/aa-ecosys/issue/AA-741/t-series-audit-end-to-end-t0t11-test-run-on-controlled-tenants-per) phase 3 (S276, `docs/audits/2026-11-10-S276-aa741-phase3/` §4.3, §5):

* **Debate:** the T7 slate Debate flags 499 of 502 candidates. In 41 of the 44 pairs with free candidates, every candidate is flagged. The 50% cut cap decides what is dropped, not the judgement.
* **Brand-fit:** the judge scores 5.9–6.3 (pass is 7), while the stored scores are 9.9–10. The core brand idea appears in only 5 of 32 texts.
* **Route order:** the judge routes start on a rung that is closed, so every call first takes a refusal before reaching the model that answers.

## Scope

* Debate becomes advisory, rank only, with no drops from its flags, until it is calibrated.
* Brand-fit and core-idea labels on the [AA-729](https://linear.app/aa-ecosys/issue/AA-729/eval-golden-set-100-samples-from-existing-data-scoring-script-for) golden set, plus a deterministic cross-brand overlap check.
* Judge routes (`t2_judge`, Debate, `t10_judge`) put the serving model first while the primary rung is closed.

## Done when

* A $0 replay of the phase 2 slates shows Debate ranking without drops.
* Brand-fit judge agreement against the labels is measured and reported, with a decision on the threshold.
* The route change is visible in route control, and the 24 h ledger shows 0 refusal rows on the judge stages.
