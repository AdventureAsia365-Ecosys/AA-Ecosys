## Goal

The AI spend controls follow the tech lead's policy (S283, 17/11/2026). They are built now, on Dev, with one code path for every environment: going to prod is a settings change, not new code.

## Policy

* **Dev:** hard caps on AI connections stay, to avoid over-testing.
* **Prod:** pay per use. No platform-wide hard cap blocks agency features, because agencies are limited by their plan.
  1. Per-tenant plan quotas (content AI budget, TripPlanner AI sessions, CRM agency AI budget) are hard. The user is told the reason clearly and gets an upgrade path.
  2. Connection caps become an alert plus failover to the next connection, and the call goes on. They never block a customer.
  3. Only an anomaly circuit breaker may stop calls. It trips at a multiple of normal daily spend; the threshold is a setting and it raises an on-call alert.
  4. AWS budgets are e-mail alerts only.
  5. Internal batch runs (waves, adhoc, eval, writer proofs) keep hard caps per run.

## Problem (S283 gap check, root `docs/audits/2026-11-17-S283-session/ai-caps-gap.md`)

* Plan quotas block silently or with a wrong message:
  * Studio shows "Writing failed".
  * TripPlanner says "Today's limit" for a monthly quota, and the plan quota seed is NULL.
  * CRM has a flat 2 USD/month budget that is not tied to the plan.
* Connection cap modes are only hard_stop or alert-only. There is no "fail over and go on" mode.
* No anomaly breaker exists. App-wide fixed USD caps block every agency at once: TripPlanner 5 USD/day, chat 2 USD/month, the CRM app daily budget.
* Waves, adhoc and eval runs have no automatic hard USD cap per run.
* Migration seeds do not depend on the environment.

## Scope (slices G1–G4, L1–L4 of the gap file)

* **G1:** a connection cap mode `failover_continue`, chosen per environment in the settings registry.
* **G2:** the anomaly breaker.
* **G3:** app-wide caps of TripPlanner and CRM become alert-only when the environment setting says prod.
* **G4:** per-run hard caps for waves, adhoc and eval.
* **L1–L4:** a clear reason and upgrade link when a quota is used up (Studio, TripPlanner, CRM); TripPlanner quota and CRM AI budget from the plan; no blocking on a read error, degrade with a warning instead.

Out of scope: TripPlanner/CRM failover routes and the prod Terraform root and config ([AA-1004](https://linear.app/aa-ecosys/issue/AA-1004/infra-implement-the-lean-aws-model-company-organization-identity)).

## Done when

* Tests prove each mode.
* On Dev the behaviour stays hard-capped.
* Flipping the environment settings on Dev shows the prod behaviour (alert + failover, breaker only), then the settings are flipped back.
* Screenshots of the quota-exhausted messages in the three apps.
