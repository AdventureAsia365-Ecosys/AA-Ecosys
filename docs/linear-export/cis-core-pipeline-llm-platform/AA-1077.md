## Problem

[AA-741](https://linear.app/aa-ecosys/issue/AA-741/t-series-audit-end-to-end-t0t11-test-run-on-controlled-tenants-per) phase 3 (S276, `docs/audits/2026-11-10-S276-aa741-phase3/` §4.5, §9.3) measured 28 blog pieces of 4 audit tenants:

* 20 pieces held, 7 approved, 1 failed.
* 13 holds come from F4 (length band 4,700–13,000 characters), and all 13 are too SHORT: median 652 words.
* The writer prompt asks for 900–1,300 words, but 25 of 28 pieces come in under 900. Length follows the seed size, not the tour length.
* The fix for a short piece is a full rewrite. 18 rewrites fixed length on only 6 pieces and added new gates on 9, for about 1.02 USD.
* The normalizer runs after the gates and shortens text without a new F4 check: 3 of the 7 approved pieces are stored under the floor.

## Scope

* One shared length floor constant for the prompt target, F4 and the normalizer. Codes `length_short` / `length_long` instead of one F4 code.
* F4 re-check after the normalizer, and a $0 re-measure of the stored pieces.
* **Extend repair:** for `length_short`, expand the thinnest sections from unused seed facts. It replaces the full rewrite. Run it on \~3 pieces first (a few cents), then on the phase 2 set.
* A decision memo for the tech lead: a floor by seed size or brand style, or a T7 minimum seed for Blog.

## Done when

* A $0 replay over the phase 2 pieces shows no approved piece stored under the floor.
* The extend repair on the 9 length-only pieces gives at least 7 approved, with 0 new holds from other gates; the cost is read from `llm_call_log`.
* The memo has the tech lead's decision.

Expected effect from the report: approved blog pieces go from 7 to up to 16 of 28, and the cost per approved piece drops from 0.55 to 0.24 USD.

Created with the tech lead's approval, S276 (10/11/2026), from [AA-741](https://linear.app/aa-ecosys/issue/AA-741/t-series-audit-end-to-end-t0t11-test-run-on-controlled-tenants-per) phase 3.
