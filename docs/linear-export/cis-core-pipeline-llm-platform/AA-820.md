## Problem

From 12/11/2026 continued use of Anthropic models on Bedrock means accepting the updated Usage Policy, which applies to "end users of products or services integrating Claude" — i.e. our tenants. Tenant Terms currently have no AUP clause. Review: AA-Ecosys docs/audits/2026-10-14-S236-design-review/aup-2026-11-review.md (S236).

## Scope (legal drafts; Claude Code prepares the points, Nghiệp/legal approve)

1. Tenant Terms clause: tenant must comply with the Anthropic Usage Policy; no fake reviews/testimonials, sockpuppets, astroturfing, fake outlet/account networks, concealed sponsorship, spam; tenant reviews and is responsible for what it publishes; AA may suspend/offboard a violating tenant (existing lifecycle [AA-797](https://linear.app/aa-ecosys/issue/AA-797/admin-tenants-bugs-status-toggle-does-nothing-plan-id-never-set)).
2. Legal confirmations: (a) T2/T9 content published under the tenant's name without an AI label does not breach the policy's origin/attribution clauses; (b) TripPlanner UI badge is sufficient AI disclosure; (c) local AI-labelling/advertising law (e.g. EU AI Act) for tenant markets.
3. Notify existing tenants of the new clause (business / Ms. Thư).
4. AA-Booking boundary: no AI advice on insurance, financing, claims or visas without qualified human review + AI disclosure (record in Booking CONTEXT).

## Done when
