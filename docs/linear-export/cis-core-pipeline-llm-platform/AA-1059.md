**25 review items vs 25 tours without a Master**

**JP/CN/TW routes**
- Gap reasons are mostly `one_day`: CN 37, JP 48, TW 10.
- The what-if "Segment on every day it appears" would add 3 tours (CN 1, LK 2), so no rule change.
- 177 ranked tours have no route: 160 one-day, 16 non-consecutive days, 7 all-excluded, 1 one-place.

**segment_gc**
- The last applied run kept 1,067 orphans and deleted 0. All 1,067 are kept by an alias, and some also by subject / brand_fit / angle request.
- Retention works as designed.

**Extra fix found by the S274 completeness audit (CIS #1413)**
- Geocoder-confirmed island places were refused as `outside_country_polygon`. Natural Earth 1:10m does not draw Koh Tao, Phi Phi or the Similans, and the polygon build dropped tiny rings such as Lakshadweep.
- The fix: an offshore rule (nearest coast within 100 km is the claimed country, and the point is in no country's polygon), plus small rings kept.
- After the deploy, 44 names were requeued (3 `outside_country_polygon` + 41 `unconfirmed_by_geocoder`). place_geocode job 3f604b27 resolved 6 names, inserted 6 destinations and relinked 7 live tour components and 3 cards. 38 stay unknown: 20 unconfirmed by the geocoder, 18 unknown to the model.

**Unknown names, whole catalogue**
- 1,471 in total: 310 are non-places by rule and 1,117 are unknown to the model (generic or obscure names).
- No pipeline gap is left.
