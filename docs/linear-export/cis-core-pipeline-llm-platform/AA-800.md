# AA-800 — [Brand pack] Tenant signature vocabulary — split the forbidden list into platform-truth vs AA-style; tenants may use their own brand words

## Problem

`VALIDATE_FORBIDDEN` (`services/content_generation/forbidden_words.py`) plus rule 1 of the writer prompt ("luxury") mix three kinds of words and apply all of them to every tenant: price/booking promises (cheap, deal, discount, book now, instant booking), stock superlatives (stunning, breathtaking, magical, paradise, pristine) and AA's own positioning taste (curated, refined, tailored, bespoke, luxury). A luxury tenant such as Atlas & Hearth cannot write in its own voice.

## Scope (approved by Nghiệp, S235 14/10/2026)

* Three layers: (1) **platform truth** — price/booking promises and unverifiable claims: blocked for every tenant; (2) **AA style** — stock superlatives + positioning words: A1 unchanged (blocked); for T2/T9 stock superlatives become a warning with a density cap (≤1 per piece), positioning words are allowed when the tenant declares them as signature vocabulary (brand pack: brand_type / voice / style guide / voice examples, or an explicit T0 field); (3) **tenant forbidden list** — unchanged, blocked.
* Evidence rule: a positioning word that asserts a service level ("luxury lodge", "tailored itinerary") must be supported by the tour data — handled by the fidelity gates (F9/F17), not the word list.
* Writer prompt rule 1 for T2/T9 built per tenant from the layers.
* Do this AFTER the S235 T2 rerun so the measurement is not confounded.

## Done when

* Unit tests per layer; A1 golden prompt unchanged; one T2 + one T9 for Atlas & Hearth uses its signature words without a forbidden-word hold, and a "luxury" claim unsupported by the tour data is flagged by the fidelity gate.
