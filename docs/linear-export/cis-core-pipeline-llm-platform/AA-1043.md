## Problem

Places are geocoded with Geoapify, an outside vendor with its own key and its own bill. Amazon Location Service (Places API: geocode, search) is the AWS-native equivalent. It would put geocoding on the AWS bill, and it needs no outside key, because access goes through IAM.

Before any switch we need evidence that it fits our places, which are Asian destinations with many local names. Two things to check:

* **Accuracy:** distance to the coordinates we already hold.
* **Price:** we store the coordinates, so calls must use `IntendedUse = Storage`, which is billed at the Stored price.

Tech lead request, S267 (04/11/2026): test on a small sample, never overwrite current data, cap the cost.

## Scope

* Read-only sample of about 50 places from the current geo tables, spread over several countries and including hard cases (islands, temples, local-language names).
* Call Amazon Location Places geocode / search-text for each place:
  * with a hard request cap in the script (≤ 120 requests in total);
  * with `IntendedUse=Storage`, so the price matches real use.
* Compare with the stored Geoapify point:
  * distance in km;
  * country match;
  * match type or confidence;
  * a hand review of the cases more than 2 km apart.
* Price per 1,000 calls for our use, from the official price page, against Geoapify's free daily credits and paid plans.
* Report in the root `docs/audits/<date>-S<nnn>-geo-location-trial/`. **No write to** `geo.*` **or any app table.**

## Done-when

* The report shows the sample results: median and p90 distance, the share within 500 m and within 2 km, country mismatches, and the hand-review notes. It also states the cost of the trial run and a monthly cost estimate at our volume.
* It ends with a recommendation (switch, keep, or use as a fallback) for the tech lead.
* Trial spend stays under 2 USD.

## Comments

### 2026-11-04 10:55 — nghiep pham quoc

**S267 trial done: keep Geoapify.**

**Setup:**
- 50 places with accepted Geoapify points, read-only sample over 9 countries, including islands, temples and non-Latin names.
- Amazon Location Places API v2 in us-west-2 (no us-west-1 endpoint), `IntendedUse=Storage`, hard cap of 120 requests.
- Result: 81 requests, **0.32 USD**. No DB or AWS resource was written.

**Results:**
- Found 44 of 50. The 6 misses are all in South Korea.
- Country match is 100 %, but the distance to the stored point is a median of 19 km and a p90 of 1,475 km.
- Hand review of the largest gaps shows the **stored Geoapify points are right and Amazon picked the wrong place by name**. For example:
  - Drepung Monastery → another monastery, 2,424 km away;
  - Ngamring lake and Wu Gorge → venues in Macao;
  - Manas NP → a park in Delhi;
  - Ikema Island → Ikoma, Nara.

**Price:** our use is Amazon's Stored bucket at 4.00 USD per 1,000 calls (Core free tier does not apply); Geoapify stays inside its free daily credits today.

**Recommendation:** keep Geoapify. Amazon Location is not worth adding even as a fallback; revisit only if Geoapify's terms or price change.

Report and scripts: root `docs/audits/2026-11-04-S267-geo-location-trial/` (in the S267 close PR).
