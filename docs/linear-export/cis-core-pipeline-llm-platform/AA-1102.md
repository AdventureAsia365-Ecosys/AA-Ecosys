## Problem

AA's lifecycle rules are spread over many files, wired to AA names and open at both ends, so a new project cannot apply them on day 1. [AA-1099](https://linear.app/aa-ecosys/issue/AA-1099/playbook-new-project-kit-standard-technical-process-governance-and) designed the kit (`docs/research/new-project-playbook/`, root #757); the tech lead approved all its decisions in S279. This issue builds the written part.

## Scope

1. R1 clean-up: move the private-memory rules (gap 7) into the handbook or skills; fix the stale rules (gap 8); accept or amend ADR 0011 and ADR 0014; update handbook D-14.
2. R2a: the new templates in `docs/templates/` and `principles.md` with the tolerance table (research §5.3), English with `.vi.md` siblings.
3. R2b: the other `docs/playbook/` pages (index, nine phases, agents, adoption, metrics, AA profile; research §5.2), English with `.vi.md`; one catalog entry in `docs/README.md`.

Out of scope: the template repo, generic skills and pilot (part 2); moving existing AA repos onto the template (D12).

## Done when

* The playbook pages, templates and catalog entry are merged on `master`, with docs lint green.
* `/admin/docs` lists the playbook pages in EN and VI.
* Lessons from the build are in the lesson log.
