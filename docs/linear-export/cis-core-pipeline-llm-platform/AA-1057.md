## Problem

From the S273 system picture (§3, §5 items 6 + 9):

* **GAP 1 (dormant, large scope: 1,237 Segment subjects).** Liveness gates follow one representative tour of a Segment. If that tour is retired while the place is still sold by another tour, the agency is blocked from writing/publishing (409), its piece is flagged, the WordPress page goes back to draft after 7 days, and it gets a wrong "no longer sold" mail.
* **GAP 2 (live, small).** Supersede/rewrite does not retarget: 7 used subjects sit on an old route, and 3 angle requests sit on deleted atoms (2 approved, 1 `pending_choice` that is stuck).

## Scope

* GAP 1: use the Subject rule (the Segment still has at least one served member) in every liveness gate, or retarget to another member.
* GAP 2: add a retarget step to supersede/rewrite; one-off repoint of the 7 subjects + 3 angle requests under the DRY RUN procedure (`skill/aa-cis-schema/references/destructive-ops.md`).
* Tests for both.

## Done-when

* A test retires a Segment's representative tour while another member is served: no 409, no flag, no mail.
* The one-off retarget ran after a DRY RUN; the propagation check reports 0 subjects on a non-live route and 0 angle requests on inactive atoms.
