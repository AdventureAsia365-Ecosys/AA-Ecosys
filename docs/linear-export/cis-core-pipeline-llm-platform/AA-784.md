# AA-784 — [Place cards] Platform-wide build — ~7–9.7k distinct places, staged by country, after the fact-kind fix (≈$15–25)

## Problem

Only \~110 cards exist (pilot tours). Platform: 24,301 active atoms / 892 tours / 9,668 distinct capitalised place|country pairs (7,065 by segment canonical place). Every T2/T9 piece on a tour without cards falls back to a thin seed — the S232–S234 data shows rich-card brand–seed pairs are the best cells.

## Scope

1. Prerequisite: the "visiting/practical facts out of the seed" issue is deployed (otherwise cards are redrafted).
2. Staged `place_cards_build` jobs (worker, ≤40 places/job), order: countries/tours that tenants use first (Bhutan, Mongolia, Laos, Nepal, India…), one trial country of \~50 places with a quality spot-check (identity, 10 cards read) before the rest.
3. Admin progress view: cards / misses / cost per country (reuse Place cards tab).
   Out of scope: re-drafting existing cards.

## Done when

* ≥90% of capitalised active-atom places have a card or a recorded miss; observed cost ≤$0.003 per place drafted and calls/new-card ≤1.2 (live `llm_call_log`); spot-check of 30 random cards: ≥27 correct identity.
