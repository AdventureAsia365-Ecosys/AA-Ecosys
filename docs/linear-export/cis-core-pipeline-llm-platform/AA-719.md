# AA-719 — [Review Queue UI] Bulk regenerate + non-blocking regenerate (UI freezes, no multi-select)

Found S209 (03/10/2026) while clearing the China/Korea/Taiwan review queue.

## Two problems

1. **No multi-select / bulk regenerate.** Each pending tour must be regenerated one at a time from its own row. With 8 pending that is 8 manual modals. Need checkbox multi-select + a "Regenerate selected" bulk action (and ideally "Regenerate all pending" with a confirm), queued through the job runner.
2. **Regenerate freezes the whole screen.** Clicking Regenerate opens a modal that runs the full pipeline synchronously; the UI then shows "Regeneration is taking too long — refresh the queue to check" and the page is frozen — you cannot click out, navigate, or use other pages until it resolves. Should be fire-and-forget: enqueue the regenerate as a job, close the modal immediately, show the tour as "regenerating" with a spinner, and let the queue refresh when the job completes (same durable-job pattern as atomize). Ties into [AA-651](https://linear.app/aa-ecosys/issue/AA-651/p1infra-worker-ecs-service-for-the-job-runner-aa-cis-infra) (move S1 rewrite onto the job runner).

## Observations (not bugs, worth noting)

* The regenerate modal says "No model tier was recorded for this tour — choose one: Haiku / Sonnet." Reasonable, but a sensible default (or remembering the last choice) would speed bulk work.
* Empirically, almost every regenerated tour passes into Master Content on the retry. Worth understanding WHY (is the rewrite genuinely better second time, non-determinism in judge/brand audit, or the first attempt hit a transient brand-audit manual_check?). Tracking as a question under this issue; may warrant a separate investigation.

## Done when

Review Queue supports multi-select bulk regenerate; a regenerate is non-blocking (job-based, modal closes immediately, no frozen screen).
