## Problem

The business owner wants to see, in as much detail as possible, how AI and data providers are used: how many requests, input/output tokens, cost, when, where, per provider, per account, per app, per stage, and more, with filters and sorting.

Today the ledger already records all of it. Every attempt in `shared.llm_call_log` has:

* time, provider, connection/account, model, app, stage, role and tenant;
* input/output/cache/reasoning tokens, cost, latency;
* status, error class, attempt number, job and tour.

What the admin shows today:

* the **Calls** tab: a list of calls with filters for window, source, app, provider, status, job and tour;
* the **Spend** page: cost per app per month, forecast and budgets;
* **Tenant 360 › Cost**;
* the tenant → model → stage cost tree.

What is missing:

* no aggregate view where you choose the dimensions and the metrics;
* no filter on stage, model, tenant or account;
* no sorting by any column;
* no time-series chart;
* no export.

Tech lead request, S267 (04/11/2026).

## Scope

* **Explorer page** under AI & spend:
  * time range presets (today, 7 days, this month, last month) plus a custom range;
  * granularity: hour, day, week or month;
  * group by 1–2 dimensions: provider, connection/account, app, product, stage, model, family, tenant, role, status, error class;
  * metrics: requests, input/output/cache/reasoning tokens, cost USD, average and p95 latency, error rate, fallback rate (attempt_no > 1 or fallback_used), cost per tour / per piece where the stage has one.
* **Filters:** multi-select on every dimension, kept in the URL so a view can be shared. Every column sortable.
* **Time-series chart** (requests, tokens, cost) above the table. A click on a cell drills down to the Calls list with the same filters, then to Trace.
* **CSV export** of the current view, and **saved views** with a name.
* **Display time in Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh**; data stays in UTC.
* **Performance:** an hourly rollup built from the ledger (job or materialised table), so the page stays fast as the ledger grows. Raw rows keep their DM-7.1 retention.
* **Sources:** the CIS ledger plus the CRM ledger (the Calls endpoint already merges both) plus any other app ledger that exists. Data providers too (DataForSEO, Geoapify, Jev) where their usage is logged.
* **Access:** admin, finance and ai_ops (R2 roles), and the business owner's account.
* **Invoice view:** each provider's ledger month total next to its invoice (ties into the [AA-1026](https://linear.app/aa-ecosys/issue/AA-1026/ai-gateway-warm-paths-route-governance-anthropic-key-onoff-runbook) reconciliation).

## Done-when

* The rollup totals equal the raw ledger totals for the same window (a test and a live check).
