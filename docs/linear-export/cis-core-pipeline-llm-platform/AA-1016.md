## Problem

Content studio tenants (B2B) have rewrite quotas that do not hard-stop and per-plan HTTP RPM, but no AI spend budget in USD. One tenant can consume a large share of the shared Bedrock quota and cost without any alert (capacity plan S261 §2.4).

## Scope

1. `tenant_ai_budget_usd` per tenant (default from the plan's price list, business owner decision B2).
2. Alert at 100 % of the plan's AI share; from 150 % new T-stage jobs wait for staff approval (no silent cut-off).
3. Count T9 pieces in the plan quota.
4. Show % used on the Tenant 360 card and on the cost page.

## Done when

- [ ] Tests: a tenant at 100 % gets the alert; at 150 % a new job waits for approval; other tenants unaffected.
- [ ] Live on Dev with a synthetic tenant (budget set low): alert and approval flow shown with screenshots.
- [ ] Cost page shows per-tenant % used.

## Deadline

Before the first paying tenant, or go-live minus 4 weeks.
