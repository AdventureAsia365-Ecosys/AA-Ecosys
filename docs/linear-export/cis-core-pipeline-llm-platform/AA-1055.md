## Problem

Search demand (Google volume and People Also Ask questions, bought from DataForSEO) is one of the four ranking axes of every Segment. It is also the only bar of the Blog channel (at least 1,000 searches and 3 questions in the tenant's market). Live on 08/11/2026 (S273):

* Only 8,662 of 97,218 current ranking rows (9%) carry a demand volume.
* In the US market, 7,116 of 7,323 places are stale (research preview).
* The research log shows 6 manual runs from 03/09 to 12/10 (about 380 US places in total), and none since.
* Cause: [AA-646](https://linear.app/aa-ecosys/issue/AA-646/p0bug-dfs-research-runs-platform-wide-after-every-tenant-rewrite-scope) made research admin-triggered only. That was right: the automatic sweep after every T2 rewrite cost too much. Nothing replaced it, though. The catalog was then re-atomized several times, which created new Segments and new place names that were never researched.
* Effect: the Blog slate of an audit tenant offered 3 Subjects from 176 Segments. 134 had no demand at all, and 119 of their 160 places had never been researched. Other channels (no bar, or the social bar) were unaffected, so nobody noticed.

## Scope

* **Budgeted refresh after a re-atomize.** When A3 creates or renames Segments, queue the new and stale places for research. The run itself still needs an explicit approval per batch (cost before run, `segment_research` budgets, at most 200 places per run).
* **Stale-share guard per market.** A daily read-only check: share of live places with no research, or research older than `FRESH_FOR`, per target market. Above a threshold, it opens or updates one issue (same pattern as the root CI hygiene issues). The admin data-coverage page shows the same number.
* **Catch-up plan for the current catalog.** Markets in tenant targets first (US, then UK/AU). About 1.7 USD per 200 places (preview estimate 08/11). US is about 36 runs, about 60 USD. Each batch is approved by the tech lead.
* The slate reports how many Blog candidates fell on "no demand data" versus "demand below the bar", so a thin Blog slate explains itself.

## Done when

* A re-atomize of one tour queues its new places for research (shown on Dev, no paid call until approved).
* The guard reports the stale share per market live, and opens the issue above the threshold.
* The slate result carries the no-data / below-bar split for Blog.
* The catch-up plan has a dated batch list with cost per batch. The first batch has run after approval.

S273 finding, add to scope: a `segment_research` run only fills the `acp_contract.search_demand` cache. `atom_ranking.demand_volume`, which the slate reads, changes only when a `recompute` job (scope platform: score + route) runs afterwards. Nothing links the two, so paid research stays invisible to the slate until some unrelated recompute happens. Fix: a successful research run enqueues one debounced platform recompute, with PAA re-landing limited to the researched Segments.

### 2026-11-08 17:28 — nghiep pham quoc

Market scope decision input (S273, live counts 09/11).

Tenants' declared buyer markets (17 tenants): agencies UK 4, AU 4, US 3, DE 3, FR 3, NL 3, CA 2, SG 2, AE 1, HK 1, NZ 1; 4 agencies declare none (default US). Only US, UK, AU, DE, FR and NL are in `DFS_LOCATION_MAP`. Unknown markets are dropped silently (`resolve_buyer_markets`); a tenant with only unknown markets falls back to US, and `shared.unmapped_market_requests` gets a row.

Proposed additions to scope:
- **Market set** = markets declared by real tenants, ordered by tenant count: US + UK + AU first, then DE/FR/NL when those agencies are live. Never "all markets".
- **Proxy map for unsupported markets** (e.g. SG/HK/AE -> UK, CA -> US, NZ -> AU), labelled as proxy in the UI and the slate reason. A real market is bought only when a paying tenant needs it; `unmapped_market_requests` is the queue.
- **Tiered purchase**: volumes for every rankable place (full 1,000-keyword tasks, about 1.7 USD per market at today's 2.6 keywords per place); PAA SERP only for places with volume >= 100 (SERP is about 47% of the cost).
- **Monthly cap** for incremental research of new places after re-atomize (e.g. 5 USD per month), with the recompute enqueued after each run. Full refresh every 6 months.
- **Relative Blog bar per market** (e.g. top quartile of that market with a floor) instead of the absolute 1,000 copied from the Japan catalogue. Product decision for the tech lead.

Cost estimate at today's measured rates: about 11-15 USD per market for the first pass, US+UK+AU about 35-45 USD once, then a few USD per month.
