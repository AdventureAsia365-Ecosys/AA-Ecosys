## Problem

S248 census (read-only, since 01/10/2026): 34 blog pieces held on F9_cta_fact (11 stopped before attempt 3) and 23 on F4_seo_surface. Diagnosis of the 3 S247 round-9 holds:

* **781b5de7**: the F9 claim sits on a markdown heading (`## What a Ruined Monastery…`). `_gate_brand_voice` treats it as a blocking body claim (the fold finds it in the body). But `sentence_repair.locate_sentence` cannot locate a heading (`hygiene.sentence_tail_end` returns None at a newline with no end mark), so `plan()` returns None and the piece is held at attempt 2. No sentence repair is tried, and no [AA-777](https://linear.app/aa-ecosys/issue/AA-777/t10-f9-judge-consistency-sticky-pass-for-unchanged-sentences-cut-truth) truth cut either. This is a code defect.
* **3712d4dd**: 4 of the 5 F9 claims are widely known background about named places, which the F9 prompt allows. The judge is over-strict, and the loop gap above makes it worse.
* **32789658**: F4_extreme_length, 14,576 characters against the blog band of 4,700–13,000, after a full rewrite at attempt 2. No trim step runs before the hold.

"Held" is an admin-only label ([AA-770](https://linear.app/aa-ecosys/issue/AA-770/t2t9-tenant-always-receives-the-content-no-heldblocked-state-scores-or)), so tenants still have the pieces. But a third of the S247 eval pieces end held, and the admin queue fills with false holds.

## Scope

1. `plan()`: when `locate_sentence` fails, match the F9 quote to a heading span from `sentence_spans`. Repair it as a heading: short, no end mark, no claim beyond the SOURCE. `splice` keeps the `## ` prefix.
2. The blog attempt-3 truth cut never deletes a heading. Its fallback is to replace the heading with the moment's place name (from `route_moments`), deterministically.
3. F4 extreme length after a rewrite: a deterministic trim to the band (drop lowest-value paragraphs, never headings or FAQ), or one length-only repair call, before holding.
4. A regression test per case, built from the three pieces' shapes.
5. Re-run the census after the deploy and compare.

## Done when

* Unit tests: a heading claim gets a repair plan (not None); the truth cut keeps headings; an over-long piece is trimmed into the band.
* Live: regenerate the 3 S248 pieces (≤ $0.5 total). None is held for these reasons, and the census of new holds over one round of ≥ 6 blog writes shows no heading-unrepairable F9.
