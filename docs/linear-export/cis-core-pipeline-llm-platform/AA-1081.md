## Problem

[AA-741](https://linear.app/aa-ecosys/issue/AA-741/t-series-audit-end-to-end-t0t11-test-run-on-controlled-tenants-per) phase 3 (S276, `docs/audits/2026-11-10-S276-aa741-phase3/` §4.3.2): 6 of 48 tour × tenant pairs got no blog topic, so no piece was written:

* 4 pairs missed the blog bar;
* 2 pairs had no proposed subject.

The agency is not told why. The slate also re-reads 60 → 142 → 183 segments as the catalogue grows.

## Scope

* A no-topic reason the agency can see per adopted tour.
* A demand-join check for low-demand tours.
* At least one Subject per adopted tour that has a free candidate.
* An incremental slate: only new or changed segments.

* A $0 replay of the phase 2 slate gives every pair with a free candidate at least one topic, and every pair without one a visible reason.
* The slate run time and segments read are reported before and after.
