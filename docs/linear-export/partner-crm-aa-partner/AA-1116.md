## Problem

The S282 progress report (G2, G5, G6, G7, G10, G11, G13, G25) found that TripPlanner and CRM are not yet sellable add-ons:

* Plan prices are still empty.
* An add-on attached later has no trial.
* A CRM agency is created by hand.
* The agency app has no TripPlanner module.
* Some CRM gates are not enforced.
* There is no invoice line for extra seats.
* TripPlanner webhook and e-mail leads are not proven live.

Content studio is the most complete add-on and is the model.

## Scope

* New tenant runs the provisioning saga with no hand steps: theme, lead channel, TripPlanner domain, CRM agency plus owner invite.
* A TripPlanner module in the agency app: site link, lead channel, theme, locale, leads, usage.
* The remaining CRM feature gates enforced.
* A seat-overage invoice line.
* TripPlanner webhook and e-mail leads proven on a synthetic tenant.
* Trial on attach, per the decision.

## Done when

A synthetic agency goes from "New tenant" to a working CRM and TripPlanner with no manual step, and its usage and invoice lines show correctly.

Source: S282 progress report and audit section 6.3. Effort L, cost 0 USD. Needs the tech lead (trial on attach) and the business owner (plan prices, G1).
