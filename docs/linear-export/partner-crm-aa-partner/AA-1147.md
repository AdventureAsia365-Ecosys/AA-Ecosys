## Problem

Agency-own lines (flight, visa, insurance, agency fee) exist as manual structured lines, but an accepted proposal does not create them, files and deadlines are not attached per line, and invoices do not show them as their own group.

## Scope (T18, round 2)

* Accepting a proposal auto-creates the agency-own lines it contains.
* Per-line attachments and deadlines; a line nearing its deadline opens a task.
* Invoice and proposal show a separate group "Services provided by <agency>" with a printed disclaimer.
* Bundling rule: only flight and agency fee may be folded into the tour price; visa and insurance are always separate lines.
* Lines do not require a supplier and show "Handled by the agency". Structured flight legs wait for pilot-agency feedback.

## Done-when

* Accepting a proposal with 1 flight and 1 visa creates 2 agency lines.
* The invoice PDF shows the agency group with the disclaimer.
* A due line opens a task; a test proves no agency line is sent to Booking.
* CI green, deployed, live check recorded here.
