## Problem

The S275 study of an Odoo tour & travel app (AA-1065, report root `docs/research/2026-11-10-odoo-tour-travel-plugin-comparison.md`) found these gaps. The tech lead approved building all of them (S275, 10/11/2026). We build the features in our own code; the app's code is OPL-1 and is never copied.

## Scope

* **Profit per trip (full P&L)** (crm-30, partly, value high, effort M).
  * Today: crm.booking_line_money / crm.v_booking_line_money_current per line (cost, sell, commission_expected, cancellation_cost); /bookings ledger with base-currency totals and margin (routers/ledger.py); margin serializer hides it from the assistant. No single trip P&L that also counts service fees, refunds, discounts, partner commissions and actual costs.
  * Build: View crm.v_trip_pnl(trip_id): revenue = issued invoices net of tax and refunds (incl. service fees, cancellation fees) ; cost = AA line cost (quote or AA invoice from crm-26) + agency line cost (actual if billed, else planned) + partner commission (crm-28) + AA cancellation_cost ; plus supplier commission income; margin, margin %, per pax. 'Money' card on the trip page for owner/agent.
  * Depends on: crm-25, crm-26, crm-28 for full numbers (works without them)
* **'Bookings with AA' page with AA invoices as the agency's actual AA cost** (crm-26, missing, value high, effort M).
  * Today: Booking contract 1.5 has GET /invoices, /invoices/{number}, /billing-status (AA-1037) and a credit stop; CRM consumer page is not built (read-ours-crm §3). AA line cost today = quote b2b_price.
  * Build: CRM page /aa-bookings: AA bookings with status, AA invoices and billing status (read live from Booking, cached in crm.aa_invoice_cache(number, status, amount, currency, due_on, booking_ids)); link each invoice to the trips' AA lines; mismatch flag when invoiced != quoted b2b_price; past-due banner before the credit stop hits a new hold.
  * Depends on: Booking agency invoices contract 1.5 (exists)
* **Supplier bills and actual vs planned cost** (crm-25, missing, value medium, effort M).
  * Today: crm.booking_line_money keeps planned cost/sell/commission_expected per line (append-only, FX frozen); no record of what the supplier actually billed.
  * Build: crm.supplier_bill(bill_id, tenant_id, agency_supplier_id, number, bill_date, currency, amount, status recorded|paid, paid_on) + crm.supplier_bill_line(bill_id, booking_line_id, amount); money kind 'actual_cost' appended to booking_line_money at bill FX; variance on the line and in the trip P&L only once billed. Owner/agent only (margin policy).
  * Depends on: crm-30
* **Profit reports by channel, product, departure month and seller + dashboard tiles** (crm-31, partly, value medium, effort M).
  * Today: /reports (api/app/reports): funnel, lost reasons, lead sources, money for owner/agent by confirmation date, team, CSV; AA cross-agency insights with k-anonymity. CRM home exists but money KPIs by lead source/product are limited.
  * Build: Extend reports with a profit cube on crm.v_trip_pnl: dimensions lead_source, sales_partner, AA tour vs ancillary, travel month, owner member; CSV. Home tiles: open proposals, deposits due this week, overdue instalments, passport/visa warnings, next departures with guaranteed status.
  * Depends on: crm-30
* **Group departure P&L with shared costs split per traveller** (crm-32, missing, value medium, effort M).
  * Today: None at departure level in the CRM; costs only sit on per-trip booking lines.
  * Build: crm.departure_cost(tenant_id, departure_ref (AA departure id or agency group code), agency_supplier_id, title, amount, currency, basis per_departure|per_pax) for costs the agency carries for its whole group (own tour leader, group flight block, welcome dinner); allocation to each trip by pax in v_trip_pnl; departure P&L on the crm-22 roster page.
  * Depends on: crm-22, crm-30
