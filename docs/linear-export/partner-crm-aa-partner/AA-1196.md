## Problem

The CRM end-to-end suite on Dev (`e2e-dev.yml` in AA-Partner, dispatched from `main`) was last green on 11/11/2026. On 27/11 (S296) it ran again and 4 flows failed. Sign-in works (the tests reach the flows), and CRM jobs that call Booking and CIS succeed, so this is not an identity or secret problem. The suite was simply not run for 16 days while the CRM and Booking changed underneath it.

Failing tests (run 45981939192, head 1402b801):

* **CRM-19 AA lines on Dev** (`tests/aa-lines.spec.ts`): waiting for an AA line status times out after 150 s.
* **CRM-21b client proposal on Dev** (`tests/client-proposal.spec.ts`): `POST proposals/{id}/blocks/{id}/options` answers **422** `agency_item_not_allowed`.
* **CRM-16 ledger and suppliers on Dev** (`tests/ledger.spec.ts`): `selectOption` times out on the owner's suppliers / payment settings page.
* **CRM-21a proposal builder on Dev** (`tests/proposal-builder.spec.ts`): a click times out (8 min).

## Goal

The CRM E2E suite is green on Dev and stays green, so a red CRM flow is found the day it breaks, not weeks later.
