Verified live:
- Setup: lead + adult companion (own e-mail) + child companion (no e-mail) on one trip. Mark paid -> Booked, invite_all invited all 3, and the three Copy link boxes appeared.
- One link: /c/t/<token> -> 303 -> /c/t. The link and session cookies are scoped to /crm/public/v1/traveller and set HttpOnly, Secure and SameSite Strict.
- Child's link: the code goes to the lead's address. POST /session without traveler_id -> 200 {signed_in:false, pick:[lead, child]}. Picking the child -> signed in, with me = child and switch_to = [lead]. The lead's link in another browser gives its own session (me = lead) while the child's stays me = child. Switching child -> lead -> child returns 200 each time. Switching to the adult -> 403 not_your_traveller.
- Child home: own flights (arrival and departure, UTC+07:00, day of trip). Only the child's own shared document and the trip-wide one are listed. There are no prices and no payments or party data; the cancelled line and the agency-only line are hidden.
- Passport scan upload -> 200 and is stored write-only. Opening it -> 403 document_write_only.
- The other traveller's document -> 403 document_not_yours. An unshared document -> 403 document_not_shared. Another agency's document -> 404 document_not_found.
- Companion invites: inviting the adult -> 409 already_invited, and his link still opens. Using the adult's address for the child -> 409 email_in_use. After the child has an own address, inviting the child -> 409 already_invited.
- The lead gives the child an own e-mail -> 200 {reach: own, link_path: null}. The note says the link went to their e-mail, and no link is shown.
- After that change, the lead-address session acting as the child is signed out (signed_in false). The child's old link returns 404 link_not_found because it was re-issued to the new address. The lead stays signed in.
- Access log (API and the Settings page) shows portal_companion_email allowed, plus refused with reason email_in_use. It also shows portal_document allowed/denied, and the pick, switch, sign-in, flights and companion-invite events.
- Screenshots of the picker, the child home and the lead's group at 1440 and 390, light and dark. No sideways overflow. The only console errors are the deliberate 403/404/409 responses.
