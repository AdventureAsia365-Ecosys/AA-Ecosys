## Problem

All CRM AI stages run on Amazon Nova Lite on acc2 (crm-042, crm-069), because the acc2 payer blocks Claude on Bedrock. The CRM was designed with Claude Haiku 4.5 then Sonnet 4.5 for `crm_draft_reply` (crm-037). Nobody has measured whether Nova Lite's client replies are good enough. The replies are read by travellers (through the agency staff), so tone and fluency matter, Vietnamese included. `crm_notes_options` and `crm_supplier_confirm` are closed-list picks and extraction checked in code; they stay on Nova Lite.

## Scope

1. A small paid comparison (tech lead S284: "làm phép so rồi nối nếu cần"): about 10 synthetic client threads (EN and VI, typical agency cases), the CRM's own `crm_draft_reply` prompt, one draft each from Nova Lite (acc2) and Claude Haiku (acc1 / Anthropic), through the CIS gateway, logged in `shared.llm_call_log` (stage `adhoc_aa<this issue>`). Cost under 0.10 USD.
2. A blind side-by-side page for the tech lead to pick the usable draft per case.
3. If Claude wins clearly: route `crm_draft_reply` (only) to Claude. The CRM has the `bedrock_role` provider kind for a cross-account role but no mapping yet; map it (or use the Anthropic API connection), then add the CRM share on that connection in `crm.ai.connection_monthly_cap_usd` and lower the CIS share so the provider budget still holds.
4. If Nova Lite is good enough: keep it, record the result.

## Done when

* The comparison result and the tech lead's picks are recorded (root audit folder).
* The route of `crm_draft_reply` matches the decision, live on Dev, with one draft proven end to end on a synthetic agency.
