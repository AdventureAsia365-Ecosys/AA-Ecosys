## Problem

Business rule (19/11/2026): all product data comes from AA and AA suppliers; agencies only use the apps. Today the CRM still lets an agency create its own suppliers and services of catalogue types (hotel, activity, DMC, transfer, guide, restaurant, "other"), and the proposal builder accepts agency options such as transfers. This breaks the rule and ADR 0019 b6.

## Scope

* B5/B9: block agency-own suppliers and lines of catalogue types (hotel incl. extra nights, all transfers and vehicles, guide, ticket, meal, activity, DMC, "other" as a loophole).
* B10/T18: the closed list of agency-own lines is flight, visa, travel insurance and agency service fee: manual, no external integration, never sent to AA.
* B11: "Ask AA for extra hotel nights" action creates a request to AA instead of an agency hotel line.
* B6: Facts Entries keep only markup and sale terms.
* T19: in the same crm-NNN migration, count first, re-type blocked `own` suppliers to `other`, move unused rows to the existing trash, keep referenced rows readable, then narrow the CHECK. No hard delete.
* Amend ADR 0019 b6 (root repo).

## Added after the cross-app permission review (S285)

Facts Entries and the agency T2 copy live in CIS, so this issue also gets a CIS PR: Facts Entries keep only booking_terms for new rows (old rows readable, no longer override AA facts); agency T2 edits refuse fact fields (fact_locked) and are audited; AA item names in CRM quotes are read-only with one agency note line. Final shape follows decisions D7–D9 on the S285 access approval page.
