## Problem

The S295 pricing audit (27/11/2026) found that agencies do not see the right price in several places: the "In my catalog" view shows raw price text instead of the computed From price; the Booking tariff has no twin-share/single-supplement split and no per-row availability; the CRM "AA prices" page lacks child, infant and single columns and an "On request" state; agencies cannot see AA's booking conditions; and contract docs and guides describe behaviour the code does not have.

## Goal

Every price an agency sees is the computed one, in the shape the market uses, with "Price on request" where there is none, and the docs match the code.

## Scope

1. **Catalog From price** (CIS + Partner web): "In my catalog" uses the `price.from.v1` lookup instead of raw price text; no price → "Price on request".
2. **Tariff shape** (Booking + contract): twin share per person + a separate single supplement; bands 8–10 and 11+ show "On request" (decision D5); availability per row; season label; a note that departures can adjust price.
3. **CRM AA prices** (Partner): child, infant and single columns, "On request" badge, "Priced tours N of M"; the page sits in the Money group of the sidebar; the studio label reads "Suggested retail (RRP)" (decision D10).
4. **CRM "Booking conditions with AA"** (Partner): terms, cancellation policy and age bands from Booking.
5. **Docs drift**: contract docs, Booking CONTEXT.md and the agency guide (+ `.vi.md`) match the code.

Out of scope: per-agency assortment, AA prices in the Content plan, owner self-approval (per-agency commercial control issue); hotel class variants; TripPlanner "Price on request" button.

## Done when

* A catalog tour with a computed price shows its From price in "In my catalog"; one without shows "Price on request" (Dev screenshot).
* The tariff API returns twin share + single supplement per row and "On request" for the 8–10 and 11+ bands (curl + contract test).
* CRM AA prices shows the new columns, the badge and the "N of M" count; the sidebar entry is under Money.
* CRM shows AA's booking conditions for a test agency.
* The docs-impact check passes and the guide no longer says "Not live yet" for live features.
