Today there is **one** M2M client (`aa-m2m-dev-crm`), and the caller is AA-Partner, AA's own CRM. Booking trusts AA-Partner's `X-AA-Tenant-Id` and only checks that the partner exists and is active. Agency isolation is enforced inside AA-Partner (per-tenant RLS).

**If agencies are later allowed to connect their own CRM** (Salesforce, HubSpot…) directly to Booking, each agency needs its **own M2M client**, bound to its `tenant_id`. The `b2b_partners.m2m_client_id` column is already there for this. Booking would then stop trusting a tenant header for those clients. Cost: Cognito M2M is about $6 per client per month plus token requests, so it scales with the number of directly connected agencies.

Decision needed (Ms. Thư): is direct agency-CRM access a product offer, and if so, is the per-agency cost passed on? Until then: one client, AA-Partner only.
