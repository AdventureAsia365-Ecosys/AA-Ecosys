## Problem

The S275 study of an Odoo tour & travel app (AA-1065, report root `docs/research/2026-11-10-odoo-tour-travel-plugin-comparison.md`) found these gaps. The tech lead approved building all of them (S275, 10/11/2026). We build the features in our own code; the app's code is OPL-1 and is never copied.

## Scope

* **Sub-agent / referral partner commission payable** (crm-28, missing, value high, effort M).
  * Today: Nothing payable: crm.commission is supplier-paid only; traveler.referrer_traveler_id and trip.lead_source record who referred, without money.
  * Build: crm.sales_partner(partner_id, tenant_id, kind sub_agent|referrer|freelancer|ota, name_enc, contact_enc, commission_type percent|fixed_per_pax|net_rate, rate, base revenue|margin, payout_due_days, active) ; trip.sales_partner_id; crm.partner_commission(trip_id, partner_id, basis_amount, amount, currency, status accrued|approved|paid|cancelled, paid_on, payout_ref) written when the trip is booked, cancelled with the trip unless kept; counted as a cost in the trip P&L; statement per partner (CSV). Owner-only approval.
  * Depends on: crm-30
* **Sub-agent portal (partners submit bookings for the agency)** (crm-29, missing, value medium, effort L).
  * Today: None. Roles are owner/agent/assistant members only (crm.member, [permissions.py](<http://permissions.py>)); the public intake form has no partner attribution.
  * Build: Per-partner signed intake link (crm.client_link kind partner_intake) that stamps sales_partner_id on the trip; light partner view (OTP sign-in like /c/t) listing their referred trips, stage, and commission status, no traveller PII beyond what they submitted. Later: let them request a quote for their client.
  * Depends on: crm-28
