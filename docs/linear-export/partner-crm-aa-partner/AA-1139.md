## Problem

A TripPlanner add-on is now provisioned, delivers leads on all three channels and enforces its AI sessions quota (proven live in S284), but the agency owner has no place in the agency app to manage it. Today the lead route, theme and locale are set by AA staff (Tenant 360) and the agency cannot see its leads or its usage. Split from the end-to-end add-on lifecycle issue (tech lead S284).

## Scope

A TripPlanner module in the agency app (CRM web / Content studio), owner and permitted roles only:

1. Site link: the agency's planner URL (and custom domain state when one exists), copy and open.
2. Lead channel: CRM (when the agency has the CRM add-on), e-mail or webhook, with the same checks CIS applies (public https host, e-mail domain rules); webhook signing key shown once / rotated.
3. Theme and locale of the planner.
4. Leads: the list of leads the planner sent, with their delivery status per channel.
5. Usage: AI sessions this month against the plan quota.

## Done when

A synthetic agency owner changes the lead channel, theme and locale from the agency app, sees a new lead and the month's AI sessions, and the planner follows the change (live, screenshots).
