### 2026-10-24 08:48 — nghiep pham quoc

Shipped (S249). Evidence:

**Review:** four rounds of independent adversarial review, each with live repro SQL on throwaway Postgres.
- Round 1 blocked on B1: a traveller erase deleted rows of a held trip or relation.
- Rounds 2 and 3 found more holes in the per-path checks: a hold reached through a co-traveller or another trip, and party edits ending a hold.
- Round 4 replaced the per-path checks with **one hold rule** (migration header "THE HOLD RULE"):
  - `hold_trip` / `hold_traveler` / `hold_row` / `erase_held`, with `legal_hold_blocks = erase_held`;
  - `LOCK TABLE crm.legal_hold IN SHARE MODE` plus a FOR UPDATE lock on the erase subject;
  - BEFORE DELETE/UPDATE triggers `crm.guard_held_change()` on 11 hold-reachable tables. Deleting, trashing or unlinking a held row is refused on every path (CRM10, mapped to 409 `legal_hold` by `tenant_tx`).
- **Round-4 verdict: approve from both lenses.** Every repro from rounds 2 and 3 is refused and is now a test case.

**Merged and deployed:**
- Migration AA-Partner #31, Infra copy #351 (cmp equal to main). DB Migrate check + apply crm/010 (runs 40078491567 / 40078599611); readiness `{"schema_version":"crm-010"}`.

**Non-blocking notes from round 4**, recorded in `docs/implementation-notes/AA-942.md`; proposed for a follow-up:
