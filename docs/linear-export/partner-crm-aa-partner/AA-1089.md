## Problem

The S275 study `docs/research/2026-11-10-odoo-tour-travel-plugin-comparison.md` ranks the CRM / Booking gaps. Ranks 45–53 are small add-ons to features we already have, for example:

* a "booking confirmed" message template;
* the cancellation policy as a merge field;
* vouchers and the visa checklist on the traveller portal;
* the source AA tour and departure on the trip header.

Approved by the tech lead in S278 (D13, NEW-C).

## Scope

Each add-on from ranks 45–53 is built in our own code, never copied from the OPL-1 app. EN by default, VI optional. Kit components only.
