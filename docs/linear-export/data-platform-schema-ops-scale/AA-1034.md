## Problem

The tech lead approved the B2B-only ecosystem model in S265 (03/11/2026). The decision page has 44 decisions, 40 of them as recommended. Record: root `docs/audits/2026-11-03-S265-ecosystem-restructure/` (README, decisions.json, approvals.json, clarifications.md, adr-0019-draft.md).

The model in short:

* AA serves tenants (agencies) only.
* CIS Content is the base plan; CRM and TripPlanner are add-ons.
* Booking becomes AA's B2B back office.
* AA sells nothing to end travellers and keeps no end-traveller data.

Today the B2C paths are still live:

* TripPlanner "Send to advisor" creates an AA B2C case in Booking.
* Booking stores B2C customer PII.
* Admin overview cards show B2C numbers.

The roadmap phase R0 (TL-15, slot S265) freezes these paths and records the decision.

## Scope

1. **ADR.** ADR 0019 "B2B-only ecosystem model" goes into root `docs/adr/` from the reviewed draft, with short amendment notes in ADRs 0001, 0004, 0005, 0005a, 0006, 0010, 0010a, 0016 and 0018.
2. **B2C paths off (Dev).**
   * A flag turns the Booking `bk_inquiry_pull` job off.
   * TripPlanner "Send to advisor" shows a demo notice instead of creating a case.
   * The B2C cards are removed from the admin overview(s).
   * A Booking migration REVOKEs INSERT on `booking.customer_*` from the app role, as the step before any later DRY RUN drop.
3. **Backlog re-scope** (`docs/backlog/README.md` + `plan.json`, TL-15):
   * CRM W6–W8 paused until after R3: [AA-960](https://linear.app/aa-ecosys/issue/AA-960/crm-w6-crm-29b-tags-system-tags-coverage-ready-to-travel), [AA-963](https://linear.app/aa-ecosys/issue/AA-963/crm-w6-crm-32-search-library), [AA-968](https://linear.app/aa-ecosys/issue/AA-968/crm-w7-crm-36-campaigns-r14), [AA-970](https://linear.app/aa-ecosys/issue/AA-970/crm-w7-crm-37b-release-train-help-data-page), [AA-980](https://linear.app/aa-ecosys/issue/AA-980/crm-w8-crm-38-go-real).
   * [AA-1003](https://linear.app/aa-ecosys/issue/AA-1003/reporting-build-the-ecosystem-report-engine-and-the-63-report-catalog) paused and re-scoped.
   * [AA-1028](https://linear.app/aa-ecosys/issue/AA-1028/platform-one-plan-catalogue-for-tenants-and-agencies-admin-crud-plan) becomes R1, the control plane.
