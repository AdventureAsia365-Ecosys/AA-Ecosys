## Problem

[AA-1001](https://linear.app/aa-ecosys/issue/AA-1001/reporting-ecosystem-report-catalog-advisory-which-reports-content) (Done S258) designed the report catalog: 63 reports in 14 families, P0 = EX-01, EX-04, EX-06, FI-01, TP-03, OPS-12, OPS-04 (docs/audits/2026-10-29-S258-report-catalog/, reading page [https://claude.ai/artifact/Jiz8KaJiWrpdrvi5eJWWJ4](<https://claude.ai/artifact/Jiz8KaJiWrpdrvi5eJWWJ4>)). Today almost no periodic report reaches its reader on its own; probes only write to job summaries; there is no KPI history. The tech lead approved building the whole catalog on 29/10/2026 (S258), in the roadmap order, starting this session.

* **R0** fix numbers first: Cost Ops acc2 budget 300 (CIS [AdventureAsia365-Ecosys/AA-CIS-App#1157](https://linear.app/aa-ecosys/review/fix-cost-ops-shows-the-acc2-budget-terraform-sets-300-usd-92125b2333ad)), register new doc IDs.

* **R2** daily safety net: OPS-12 digest, TP-03 lead check, DATA-01 probe history, OPS-04 postmortems (first: acc3 access incident S257).

  Out of scope: paid BI tools; data that needs new app schemas before its slice (tp-/bk- sources in R7).
