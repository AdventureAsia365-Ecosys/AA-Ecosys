## Problem

Platform › Compliance readiness scores 6 security and AI frameworks, but not the personal data laws.

The repo already holds most of the privacy evidence:

* RoPA register
* DPA and 4 annexes
* DSAR erasure runbook, with two real erasure drills
* retention log
* consent capture
* Vietnam cross-border transfer dossier

The admin cannot see which obligations are met and which are not, and nobody has written down which countries' laws apply.

## Decision (tech lead, S287 close)

Q14 changed: add the Vietnam personal data law and GDPR now, in S288. It replaces "after the pilot".

The tech lead also asked about the other international laws. They are in scope here, but only after an applicability check: list a law only when it applies to AA by market and threshold.

They are legal obligations, so they show as a met / not met checklist. They are not a percentage score. Each item that needs a lawyer carries a "counsel review" flag. Any legal reading stays with the business owner.

## Scope

**1. Applicability matrix by market** (a short private register, readiness not legal advice).

* Rows: where AA processes personal data. That covers travellers from the CIS markets (US, UK, AU, DE, FR, NL) and agencies (tenants) and their clients.
* Columns: the candidate laws, the applicability test or threshold, a primary-source link, and verdict applies / not yet / not applicable.
* Candidates to assess:
  * UK GDPR + Data Protection Act 2018.
  * Australia Privacy Act 1988 (APPs) and its 2024 amendments.
  * US state privacy laws (CCPA/CPRA and others), which apply only above their thresholds.
  * Marketing and anti-spam rules for CRM e-mail campaigns: EU ePrivacy / national rules, UK PECR, Australia Spam Act, US CAN-SPAM.
  * Vietnam Data Law 60/2024 and the data localisation rules of the cybersecurity law.
  * PCI DSS, only once online card payments are switched on (already a phase 4 trigger).

**2. Obligation sets in the compliance register**

* Vietnam: Law on Personal Data Protection 91/2025/QH15 and Decree 356/2025/ND-CP, in force since 01/01/2026.
* GDPR: the articles that apply to a processor or controller of EU travellers' data.
* Every other law the matrix marks "applies".
* Each obligation has an article reference, mapped to existing controls where one fits.

**3. Page**

* An "Obligations" tab or section on Platform › Compliance readiness: per law, items met / not met / not yet checked, a counsel flag and the evidence link.
* The page also shows the applicability matrix with its verdicts.

**4. Evidence**

* From the existing documents in `docs/legal/` (link and last review date).
* From automatic checks where possible: DSAR runbook drill age, retention runs, sub-processor list update date, consent capture present, campaign sends only to opted-in contacts.
* Evidence never contains customers' personal data. Staff identifiers only.

## Done when

* The applicability matrix exists, with a primary source for each verdict, and is shown on the page.
* The Vietnam, GDPR and every "applies" checklist show on the page live, each item with its state, counsel flag and evidence link.
* At least the evidence-backed items (RoPA, DPA, DSAR drill, retention, transfer dossier, campaign consent) read "met" from a real evidence row.
* Thresholds and validity windows are settings.
* No UI text claims legal compliance. The page says "readiness, not legal advice".
