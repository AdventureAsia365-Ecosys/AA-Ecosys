## Context

Found during the [AA-605](https://linear.app/aa-ecosys/issue/AA-605/uiux-djong-bo-phong-cach-admin-portal-voi-trang-chinh-adventureasia) logged-in production check of the tenant portal (WanderLux, 25/09/2026). Each item below makes the portal look unfinished or leaks internals to a paying customer.

## Scope

1. **Quota reset date is hardcoded.** DashboardTab shows the literal "Resets in 22 days". Compute it from the billing period: days until the first of next month.
2. **Dead top-bar controls.**
   * The bell button has no handler.
   * The search box only jumps to Browse Tours and does not carry the query.
   * Wire the bell to a real notifications panel (see the Portal polish issue).
   * Make search pass `?q=` to Browse Tours and filter there.
3. **Internal cost and jargon shown to tenants.**
   * "LLM cost $0.0000" with 4 decimals and a "Low LLM spend" chip.
   * "\~$0.018/tour · Bedrock".
   * "browse the pool".
   * Tenants should see usage against quota and their plan price, not model names or per-call LLM cost.
4. **Missing page headers.** Dashboard, Browse Tours and My Catalog Tours have no h1 / description, while every other portal page has one. Use one shared page-header component.

## Done when

* Merged, CI green, Vercel production success.
* Logged-in production check at 1440px and 400px: every portal page has a header, no internal cost or model names are visible, the reset date matches the calendar, and the bell and search both work.
