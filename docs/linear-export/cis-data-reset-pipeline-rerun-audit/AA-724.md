# AA-724 — [S1 writer] Cycle/bike tours fail brand-fit — writer emits hype/persona tone + forbidden words, regenerate can't fix

## Problem (found S212, Thailand rerun, 05/10/2026)

Two Thailand cycling tours stayed in the review queue after **4 regenerate attempts each** — they never pass, for the same reason every version:

* `Thailand – Cambodia: Villages and Temples Cycle Tour (2026)` — v4, overall 5.0, judge brand_fit 5.0
* `Thailand – Bangkok to Phuket by Bike (2026)` — v4, overall 3.0, judge brand_fit 3.0

## Root cause (not a thin-raw-data case — raw is full, 9.7k–10.9k char itineraries)

The sub-scores are all high (brand 10, seo 9.5, structure 10, quality 8.5–9) — content quality is fine. **The S1 judge (s1_judge) scores brand_fit low (3–5)** because the writer renders a **persona-led, marketing/hype tone** that a neutral master catalog must not have. Judge feedback quotes the exact phrases: “Sunglasses on”, “made for riding”, “on your own terms”, “smooth bike-friendly roads that seem built for exactly what we’re doing”, “the kind of route that justifies an early wake”, “playlist-worthy roads”. Plus `FORBIDDEN_WORD` and `SEO_META_TOO_LONG` on every attempt.

`score_overall = min(validate, judge)` so the low judge drags overall under the gate. Regenerating with the same model + prompt keeps producing the same adventure-operator voice — **regenerate cannot fix it**; it is a writer-prompt problem, specific to adventure/cycle/bike tours whose raw copy is itself promotional.

## Scope

* S1 writer (`s1_generate`) prompt: steer toward a neutral master-catalog voice; strip persona/hype framing even when the SOURCE copy is promotional (these cycle tours' raw itineraries are marketing-heavy, and the writer is echoing that).
* Tie in the deterministic forbidden-word guard (same discipline as [AA-641](https://linear.app/aa-ecosys/issue/AA-641/t2-pipeline-stop-flag-fix-from-creating-new-hard-failures) for T2, but this is the A1/S1 admin path).
* Consider a targeted `s1_flag_fix` nudge for the hype/persona codes so a borderline tour self-corrects instead of looping the review queue.
